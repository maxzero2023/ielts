11月03日 午间阅读 G类 Section 1

来源：自编练习。官方免费 G 类样题前几天已经做完，所以按 G 类 Section 1 的风格自己写（日常生活类：五家搬家公司广告、诊所预约新规通知），题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。今天是第 2 周第 5 天，基础期，不限时精读，参考用时 20 分钟。

做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：信息匹配（1–7，选是哪家公司，字母可以重复用）和笔记填空（8–14）。都是 Section 1 高频题。
  2. 匹配题方法：先把 7 个题干的关键词圈出来（store、age、insurance、short notice），再一家一家读广告，读到就回去对号，不要一题一题来回翻全文。
  3. 8–14 字数限制是 ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER，只填一个词或一个数字，超字数算错。题目里已经给了的词（比如 hours）不要重复写。
  4. 小心干扰：同一段里会出现好几个数字（200、24、three），还有 doctor 和 nurse 这种角色替换，看清题目问的是谁、是哪个数。
  5. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 被相反信息干扰 / 超字数 / 看错对象。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Questions 1–7

Look at the five advertisements A–E in Text 1. For which company are the following statements true? Choose the correct letter A–E. NB You may use any letter more than once.

  1. Customers can store their belongings with this company for some time.
  2. This company gives customers boxes without charging for them.
  3. Customers have to be above a certain age.
  4. The price includes insurance.
  5. This company only works outside normal office hours.
  6. Someone will inspect your home before you are told the price.
  7. This company can often move people at very short notice.

Questions 8–14

Complete the notes below. Choose ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER from Text 2 for each answer.

Riverside Medical Centre: new system

  1. Routine appointments can no longer be booked at
  2. Phone bookings can be made from
  3. For same-day appointments, a will phone the patient back.
  4. The call back will come within hours.
  5. Paper prescription requests can be put in a at the main entrance.
  6. Prescriptions take working days to be ready.
  7. Cancel at least hours before the appointment.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：…your goods can be kept in our secure warehouse for up to six months before shipping.

store = be kept，for some time = up to six months。

2. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Boxes, tape and other packing materials are provided free of charge…

without charging = free of charge。别选 C，C 是自己装车，跟免费箱子无关。

3. C（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Customers must be over 25…

above a certain age = over 25。

4. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…every move is fully insured at no extra cost.

price includes insurance = insured at no extra cost。

5. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Moves are available in the evenings and at weekends only…

outside normal office hours = evenings and weekends only，关键是 only。

6. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：A free home survey is carried out before we give you a quote.

inspect your home = home survey，price = quote。D 可以重复用。

7. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…can usually offer a booking within 48 hours.

at very short notice = within 48 hours。B 要提前三周，正好相反。

8. reception（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Patients will no longer be able to book routine appointments by turning up at reception.

no longer = can no longer，填 reception。

9. 8 am / 8am / 8（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…appointments can be made by phone from 8 am…

填 8 am，注意别填 online，那是另一种方式。

10. nurse（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：A nurse will call you back within two hours…

phone back = call back，打回来的是 nurse 不是 doctor，这是常见陷阱。

11. two / 2（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…within two hours…

题目后面已经有 hours，只填 two 或 2。

12. box（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…dropping the request form into the box at the main entrance.

put in = drop into，填 box。

13. three / 3（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：Please allow three working days for your prescription to be ready.

填 three 或 3，working days 题目已经给了。

14. 24 / twenty-four / twenty four（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：…please cancel at least 24 hours beforehand…

beforehand = before the appointment，填 24。别被 200 和 three 干扰。
