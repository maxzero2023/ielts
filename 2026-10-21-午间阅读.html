NORTH HARBOUR STADIUM

A There are eight gates surrounding围绕…的 the stadium, six of which are entry points入口. Your ticket will indicate标明；指出 the best location for you to use with the easiest access to your seat. Public access is restricted受限制的 to Gates A to F.

B It is usual for visitors to be permitted to被允许 gain entry进入（场馆） to the stadium at least an hour and a half before the main event. Your ticket will indicate when you can enter the gate to the event you are attending. If you have any concerns, please call the stadium office on 414 0398.

C North Harbour Stadium provides food and beverage饮料 services to all areas of the stadium. All catering outlets餐饮供应点 are situated with clear views of the stadium so you can keep up to date with随时了解 the on-field action.

D Everyone entering the stadium with a package or bag may be asked to agree to an inspection检查. Please visit our website at www.stadium.co.nz for a list of the prohibited items违禁物品 stadium staff are required to search for. Visitors who refuse an inspection will not be permitted to attend the event.

E The ticketing agent will inform you of the current ticket prices for the event you wish to attend. For most events, children three years and under are free when seated on the knee of an accompanying陪同的 paying parent or guardian监护人.

F To encourage visitors to travel to the stadium without their cars, a park and ride scheme停车换乘方案（把车停在别处再坐公交） is provided. The scheme offers you the opportunity to leave your car at various locations in the central city and travel out to the stadium by bus, where you will be dropped off within walking distance在步行可达的范围内 of the gate entrance as indicated on your ticket.

G It is important that all visitors to the stadium enjoy the entertainment and services on offer in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere. Anyone causing disruption扰乱；干扰 to others will be removed from the area and in some cases asked to leave the premises（场馆）场地；房屋.

Text 2（Questions 8–14）

RIDELINE INFORMATION SERVICE

Rideline is Auckland’s free public transport information service. Rideline will help you with timetable and fare车费；票价 information on transport services within the city. For bus and train services to other regions, phone the Intercity Information Service on 913 6100.

Most operators运营商 on all forms of public transport in the Auckland region use smart cards, a credit-card-sized plastic card. Once purchased they can be used as multi-journey可多次乘坐的 monthly tickets. Drivers also accept cash for payment of fares. Exact change正好的零钱（不用找零） is appreciated but small notes can usually be accepted and change given.

Fares are charged according to the distance you wish to travel. ‘Stage points’ are used in Auckland to calculate the distance to be charged. Stage points are set every four kilometres at specific points such as a local shopping centre. As you travel from one stage point to the next, your fare increases.

School pupils aged 5 to 15 travel for a child fare which is 60% of the adult fare. Students aged 16 to 18 must present the driver with a valid有效的 school ID card in order to obtain获得 a child fare. University students can buy multi-journey tickets at a 20% discount from on-campus校园内的 locations only. A valid student ID card must also be shown to the driver when boarding the vehicle.

Senior citizen老年人（通常65岁以上） fares are available to Auckland residents居民 aged 65 and over. To obtain this fare, a valid Senior Citizen ID card must be presented to the driver. Senior citizen fares are not available before 9am on weekdays.

It is recommended that visitors to Auckland purchase a Discovery Pass which covers the cost of all travel on public transport within the city for twenty-four hours.

Questions 8–14

Do the following statements agree with the information given in the text?
TRUE 与原文一致　FALSE 与原文矛盾　NOT GIVEN 原文没提到

  1. Rideline gives advice on transport services in cities throughout the country.
  2. Passengers are able to purchase a smart card from the driver.
  3. Drivers can only accept the exact amount of money for a cash fare.
  4. Stage points are evenly spaced along the route.
  5. Children under five years old travel free on buses and trains.
  6. University students can obtain discount cards from the driver.
  7. The Discovery Pass gives a person an unlimited day’s travel within Auckland.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. vii Stadium entrances

原文：There are eight gates surrounding the stadium, six of which are entry points.

gates / entry points = entrances，整段讲从哪个门进场。

2. iv Admission times

原文：It is usual for visitors to be permitted to gain entry to the stadium at least an hour and a half before the main event.

讲“什么时候可以进场”= admission times（admission = 入场）。

3. ix Refreshments

原文：North Harbour Stadium provides food and beverage services to all areas of the stadium.

food and beverage = refreshments（饮食、茶点），同义替换。

4. x Security procedures

原文：Everyone entering the stadium with a package or bag may be asked to agree to an inspection.

包裹检查、违禁物品 = security procedures（安检程序）。

5. ii Entrance charges

原文：The ticketing agent will inform you of the current ticket prices for the event you wish to attend.

ticket prices = entrance charges（入场费用）；注意不是 i Ticket collection（取票）。

6. iii Public transport

原文：…leave your car at various locations in the central city and travel out to the stadium by bus…

车停在市中心、坐巴士去场馆 = public transport；干扰项 v Stadium car parks——车并不停在体育场。

7. viii Public behaviour

原文：Anyone causing disruption to others will be removed from the area…

讲观众要守秩序、扰乱他人会被请出 = public behaviour。

8. FALSE

原文：Rideline will help you with timetable and fare information on transport services within the city. For bus and train services to other regions, phone the Intercity Information Service…

Rideline 只管市内（within the city），外地要打另一个电话，与“全国城市”矛盾 → FALSE。

9. NOT GIVEN

原文：Most operators … use smart cards… Once purchased they can be used as multi-journey monthly tickets. Drivers also accept cash for payment of fares.

原文只说司机收现金买单程票，没说在哪买智能卡 → NOT GIVEN（别把“司机收钱”脑补成“司机卖卡”）。

10. FALSE

原文：Exact change is appreciated but small notes can usually be accepted and change given.

原文说小面额纸币也收、会找零，与 only exact amount 矛盾 → FALSE。

11. TRUE

原文：Stage points are set every four kilometres at specific points such as a local shopping centre.

every four kilometres = evenly spaced（等距分布）→ TRUE。

12. NOT GIVEN

原文：School pupils aged 5 to 15 travel for a child fare which is 60% of the adult fare.

只提到5–15岁付儿童票，5岁以下没说；“3岁以下免费”出自体育场那篇，是串文干扰 → NOT GIVEN。

13. FALSE

原文：University students can buy multi-journey tickets at a 20% discount from on-campus locations only.

only 校园内能买，不能找司机买 → FALSE（司机只是查看学生证）。

14. TRUE

原文：…a Discovery Pass which covers the cost of all travel on public transport within the city for twenty-four hours.

all travel … for twenty-four hours = unlimited day’s travel → TRUE。
