做题要求
  1. 今天三种题型：单选题（1–6，对应 Text 1）、段落标题匹配（7–10，Text 2 的 E–H 段，7 个标题选 4 个，有 3 个干扰项）、流程图填空（11–14，对应 I 段，每空 ONE WORD ONLY）。
  2. 单选题：先读题干划关键词，回原文定位，再逐个排除选项。和原文意思“差一点”的选项通常就是陷阱，比如时间不对、already 和 next year 不对。
  3. 标题匹配：每段先读首句和尾句，概括这段在讲什么，再去标题里找。别因为标题里有段落里出现过的词就选。
  4. 流程图：按顺序找，抄原词，只填一个词。
  5. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 被原文同词干扰 / 只看了一句就选 / 拼写。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Text 1：Northbridge City Museum: Volunteer Guides Wanted

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号（A–I）可单独切换该段翻译

A Who we are looking for
Northbridge City Museum welcomes around 250,000 visitors a year, and our volunteer guides play a vital role至关重要的作用 in making each visit memorable难忘的. You do not need any formal qualifications正式学历/资格证书 in history or art. What matters most is that you enjoy talking to people and are willing to learn. Volunteers must be at least 18 years old, and we ask everyone to commit to承诺做 a minimum of one half-day shift every fortnight两周 for at least a year.

【A 段译文】诺斯布里奇市博物馆每年接待约25万名游客，我们的志愿讲解员在让每一次参观都难忘这件事上起着至关重要的作用。你不需要任何历史或艺术方面的正式学历。最重要的是你喜欢和人交谈，并且愿意学习。志愿者必须年满18岁，我们要求每个人承诺至少每两周值一次半天班，并坚持至少一年。

B Training
Before leading any tours, all new guides attend four evening sessions, held on Tuesdays from 6 pm to 8.30 pm. These cover the history of the building, the main collections and how to deal with应对；处理 large groups. After this, you will accompany陪同 a senior guide on three tours before taking your first group alone. Refreshments茶点 are provided at every session, and travel expenses of up to £10 per session can be claimed back报销.

【B 段译文】在带任何参观团之前，所有新讲解员都要参加四次晚间培训课，时间是每周二晚上6点到8点半。内容包括这座建筑的历史、主要藏品，以及如何应对大型团体。之后，你要陪同一位资深讲解员带三次参观，然后才独自带第一个团。每次培训都提供茶点，每次最多10英镑的交通费可以报销。

C What the role involves
Most tours last around 45 minutes and are given to groups of no more than 20 people. Guides on the morning shift mainly work with school parties学校团体, while afternoon tours are usually for adult visitors and tourists. Some guides also help at special events, such as late-night openings, but this is entirely voluntary完全自愿. If you speak a second language, please tell us, as we are hoping to offer tours in Spanish and Mandarin next year.

【C 段译文】大多数参观大约持续45分钟，每团不超过20人。上午班的讲解员主要接待学校团体，而下午的参观通常面向成年访客和游客。有些讲解员也会在特别活动中帮忙，比如夜间开放日，但这完全是自愿的。如果你会说第二门语言，请告诉我们，因为我们希望明年推出西班牙语和普通话讲解。

D Benefits
Although the role is unpaid无报酬的, volunteers receive free entry to all paid exhibitions and a 15% discount in the museum shop and café. Each guide is also invited to an annual每年一次的 thank-you dinner in December. After two years of regular service, guides can request a written reference推荐信, which many have found useful when applying for jobs.

【D 段译文】虽然这个岗位没有报酬，但志愿者可以免费参观所有收费展览，并在博物馆商店和咖啡馆享受八五折优惠。每位讲解员还会受邀参加每年十二月的感谢晚宴。定期服务满两年后，讲解员可以申请一封书面推荐信，很多人发现这在求职时很有用。

Text 2：Kingsley Insurance: Cycle to Work Scheme

E The scheme is open to all permanent employees正式员工 who have passed their probation period试用期. Staff on temporary contracts are not eligible有资格的, and you must be aged 18 or over. Because the cost is taken from your salary, your monthly pay after the deduction扣款 must not fall below the national minimum wage最低工资.

【E 段译文】该计划面向所有已经通过试用期的正式员工。临时合同员工没有资格参加，而且你必须年满18岁。因为费用是从你的工资里扣的，扣款后的月工资不能低于国家最低工资标准。

F You can choose any bike from one of our two partner shops, Wheelworks and City Cycles, up to a value of £1,500. Helmets, lights and locks can also be included in the package, but clothing cannot. Once you have chosen, the shop will give you a quote报价单, which you should upload to the scheme website.

【F 段译文】你可以在我们的两家合作车行 Wheelworks 和 City Cycles 中任选一辆自行车，价值最高1500英镑。头盔、车灯和车锁也可以算进套餐里，但骑行服不行。选好之后，车行会给你一份报价单，你需要把它上传到计划的网站上。

G The total cost is divided into twelve equal monthly payments, which are deducted from your salary before tax税前. This means that most staff save between 25% and 39% compared with buying the same bike in a shop. Payments begin in the month after you collect your bike.

【G 段译文】总费用分成十二笔等额的月付款，在税前从你的工资中扣除。这意味着和在店里直接买同一辆车相比，大多数员工能省下25%到39%。付款从你取车后的下一个月开始。

H If you stop working for Kingsley before all twelve payments have been made, the remaining amount剩余金额 will be taken from your final salary. You will still keep the bike. Please note that the bike cannot be sold while you are still making payments.

【H 段译文】如果你在付完全部十二笔款项之前离开 Kingsley，剩余金额将从你最后一个月的工资中扣除。自行车仍然归你。请注意，在你还在付款期间，自行车不能卖掉。

I How to apply
First, make sure you meet the conditions above, then visit a partner shop to try out some bikes. Ask the shop for a written quote and upload it to the scheme website. Your line manager直属经理 will then be sent an email asking them to approve批准 your application. Once approval is given, you will receive a certificate by email, which you take to the shop when you collect your bike. Finally, remember to sign the hire agreement租用协议 on the website within 14 days.

【I 段译文】首先，确认你符合上述条件，然后去一家合作车行试骑几辆车。向车行要一份书面报价单，并上传到计划网站。之后你的直属经理会收到一封邮件，请他批准你的申请。批准之后，你会通过邮件收到一张证明，取车时把它带到车行。最后，记得在14天内在网站上签署租用协议。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. C（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：What matters most is that you enjoy talking to people and are willing to learn.

most important = what matters most，talking to people 就是 contact with people，选 C。A 原文明确说不需要。

2. B（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…a minimum of one half-day shift every fortnight…

fortnight = 两周，选 B。这个词考试常考，一定记住。

3. C（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…you will accompany a senior guide on three tours before taking your first group alone.

accompany a senior guide = go with an experienced guide，选 C。A 错在是 evening，B 错在交通费能报销。

4. B（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Guides on the morning shift mainly work with school parties…

school parties = school groups，选 B。party 这里是“团体”不是“聚会”。

5. C（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…we are hoping to offer tours in Spanish and Mandarin next year.

hoping to … next year = plans to，选 C。B 错在 already，明年才有。

6. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：After two years of regular service, guides can request a written reference…

只有推荐信有“两年”的条件，选 D。其他福利一开始就有。

7. iii（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：The scheme is open to all permanent employees… Staff on temporary contracts are not eligible…

8. ii（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：You can choose any bike… Helmets, lights and locks can also be included…

讲选车和配件，extra equipment = helmets, lights and locks，选 ii。

9. i（出处：Paragraph G）

讲钱怎么扣，选 i。

stop working for = leave the company，选 vi。iv、v、vii 原文都没讲，是干扰项。
