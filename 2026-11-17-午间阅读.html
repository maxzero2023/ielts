来源：自编练习。官方免费 G 类样题已经用完，所以按 G 类 Section 2 的风格自己写（职场类：保险公司新员工须知、办公楼消防安全规定），题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。今天是第 4 周第 5 天，基础期最后一天，不限时精读，参考用时 20 分钟。明天进入强化期，阅读会加计时器。

做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：段落标题匹配（1–6，给 Text 1 的 A–F 段选标题，i–ix 共 9 个，有 3 个用不到）、句子填空（7–14，对应 Text 2，每空 ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER）。
  2. 标题匹配：每段先读首句和尾句，抓“这段整体在讲什么”，别被段里的单个细节词带跑。选之前问自己：这个标题能不能概括整段？
  3. 句子填空：先看空前空后判断词性，再回原文定位。只能填一个词，空前空后已经有的词不要重复写。注意题干里藏着的信息，比如第 10 题。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：只看细节没看大意 / 同义替换没认出 / 超字数或重复 / 拼写。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Text 1：Brightwater Insurance: Information for New Staff

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号可单独切换该段翻译

A On your first morning, please report to the main reception desk at 8.45 am, where a member of the security team保安部 will take your photograph for your staff pass. You will then spend the rest of the day with someone from the Human Resources人力资源部 department, who will show you around the building, explain the company's history and introduce介绍 you to the people you will be working with most closely.

【A 段译文】第一天早上，请在上午8点45分到主前台报到，保安部的同事会在那里给你拍照，用来做员工证。之后这一整天你都会和人力资源部的一位同事在一起，他会带你参观大楼、介绍公司的历史，并把你介绍给你今后合作最密切的同事。

B Spaces in the staff car park are limited, and permits许可证（停车证） are given first to employees who share a car with at least one colleague. If you do not drive, the number 42 bus stops directly outside the building every fifteen minutes. Staff who cycle can leave their bikes in the covered shelter有顶棚的车棚 beside the side entrance, and showers are available on the ground floor.

【B 段译文】员工停车场车位有限，停车证优先发给至少和一位同事拼车的员工。如果你不开车，42路公交车每15分钟一班，就停在大楼正门外。骑车上班的员工可以把自行车停在侧门旁边有顶棚的车棚里，一楼还有淋浴间。

C All full-time staff are entitled to有权享有 a 45-minute lunch break, which should be taken at some point between 12 noon and 2.30 pm by arrangement with经……商定 your supervisor主管. The staff restaurant on the third floor serves hot meals and salads at prices that are subsidised有补贴的 by the company. Alternatively或者；另外, there is a small kitchen on every floor with a fridge and a microwave if you prefer to bring your own food.

【C 段译文】所有全职员工都有权享受45分钟的午休，具体在中午12点到下午2点半之间的哪个时段休息，要和你的主管商定。三楼的员工餐厅供应热菜和沙拉，价格有公司补贴。另外，如果你更想自己带饭，每层楼都有一个带冰箱和微波炉的小厨房。

D We do not expect staff to wear a suit, but you should look smart整洁得体的 and professional at all times. Trainers, shorts and T-shirts with slogans are not acceptable可接受的. On Fridays you may wear jeans, unless you are meeting customers or visitors from outside the company, in which case在那种情况下 normal business clothing is required.

【D 段译文】我们不要求员工穿西装，但你应该始终保持整洁、职业的形象。运动鞋、短裤和印有标语的T恤都是不允许的。周五可以穿牛仔裤，但如果你要见客户或公司外部的访客，就必须穿正常的商务服装。

E Every employee is given a locker储物柜 in the basement, and you will receive the key on your first day. Please do not leave cash or valuable贵重的 items on your desk, as the company cannot accept responsibility承担责任 for anything that is lost or stolen. Any items that are found in the building are handed in上交 to reception, where they are kept for one month.

【E 段译文】每位员工在地下室都有一个储物柜，第一天你就会拿到钥匙。请不要把现金或贵重物品放在办公桌上，因为丢失或被盗的物品公司概不负责。在大楼里捡到的东西都会交到前台，在那里保管一个月。

F If you are too unwell to come to work, you must telephone your line manager直属经理 before 9.30 am on the first day of your absence缺勤. Sending an email or a text message is not enough. You should keep your manager informed each day until you return. For any absence of more than seven days, you will need to provide a note from your doctor.

【F 段译文】如果你病得不能来上班，必须在缺勤第一天上午9点半之前给你的直属经理打电话。只发邮件或短信是不够的。你应该每天告知经理你的情况，直到回来上班。缺勤超过七天的，需要提供医生开的证明。

Text 2：Fire Safety at Brightwater House

G The fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 11 am. During the test, the alarm sounds for about ten seconds and there is no need to leave the building. If the alarm rings continuously持续不断地 at any other time, you must leave immediately by the nearest staircase. Never use the lifts, and do not stop to collect your coat or bag.

【G 段译文】火警警报每周三上午11点测试一次。测试时警报大约响十秒钟，不需要离开大楼。如果其他时间警报持续不停地响，你必须立刻从最近的楼梯离开。千万不要坐电梯，也不要停下来拿外套或包。

H Each floor has a fire warden消防引导员, who will be wearing a bright yellow jacket. Wardens are responsible for checking that toilets and meeting rooms are empty before they leave the floor themselves. Everyone should then go to the assembly point集合点, which is in the visitors' car park opposite the main entrance. Stay there until the fire safety manager tells you that it is safe to go back inside.

【H 段译文】每层楼都有一名消防引导员，他们会穿着亮黄色的马甲。引导员负责在自己离开本层之前，检查厕所和会议室里有没有人。然后所有人都应该去集合点，集合点在正门对面的访客停车场。待在那里，直到消防安全经理告诉你可以安全返回大楼。

I All new employees must complete an online fire safety module（课程）单元 within their first two weeks. Fire extinguishers灭火器 are located next to each staircase, but they should only be used by staff who have been trained and only if the fire is very small. A full practice evacuation疏散 is held twice a year, and staff are not told the date in advance提前.

【I 段译文】所有新员工都必须在入职头两周内完成一个在线消防安全课程单元。灭火器放在每个楼梯口旁边，但只有受过培训的员工才能使用，而且只能在火很小的时候用。公司每年举行两次全面的疏散演练，员工不会提前得知日期。

  5. Before leaving, wardens make sure nobody is in the toilets or rooms.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. i（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…show you around the building, explain the company's history and introduce you to the people you will be working with most closely.

company's history = the organisation，people you will be working with = your team，选 i。拍照做员工证只是细节，不是段落大意。

2. ii（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…the number 42 bus stops directly outside… Staff who cycle can leave their bikes…

整段讲开车、公交、骑车三种上班方式，ways of reaching the workplace，选 ii。

3. iv（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…a 45-minute lunch break… The staff restaurant… a small kitchen on every floor…

讲午休时段和在哪吃饭，time and places to have a meal，选 iv。别被 between 12 noon and 2.30 pm 误导去选 ix，那是午休时间，不是改工作时间。

4. v（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：Trainers, shorts and T-shirts with slogans are not acceptable. On Fridays you may wear jeans…

讲能穿什么不能穿什么，suitable = acceptable，选 v。

5. vi（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Please do not leave cash or valuable items on your desk…

possessions = cash or valuable items / your things，讲储物柜和失物，选 vi。

6. viii（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：If you are too unwell to come to work, you must telephone your line manager…

ill = unwell，informing the company = telephone your line manager，选 viii。iii 和 vii 原文没讲，是干扰项。

7. wednesday（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：The fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 11 am.

checked = tested，once a week = every，11 am 对应 morning，填 Wednesday。

8. ten（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…the alarm sounds for about ten seconds…

approximately = about，填 ten（写 10 也对）。

9. lifts（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：Never use the lifts…

must not take = never use，填 lifts。

10. jacket（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…a fire warden, who will be wearing a bright yellow jacket.

题目说 colour of their ___，颜色已经在题干里了，填的是名词 jacket，不是 yellow。这是今天最容易错的一题。

11. meeting（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…checking that toilets and meeting rooms are empty…

make sure nobody is in = checking… are empty，空后已经有 rooms，只填 meeting。

12. entrance（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…the visitors' car park opposite the main entrance.

across from = opposite，空前有 main，填 entrance。

13. module（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：All new employees must complete an online fire safety module within their first two weeks.

finish = complete，填 module。只能填一个词，fire safety module 超字数。

14. twice（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：A full practice evacuation is held twice a year, and staff are not told the date in advance.

take place = is held，without warning = not told the date in advance，填 twice。
