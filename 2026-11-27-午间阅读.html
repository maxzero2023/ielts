做题要求
  1. 先点“开始计时”，15 分钟内做完 14 题。时间到计时条变红，继续做完，记下超了多少。做题时先别看虚线词和翻译。
  2. 两种题型：TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN 判断（1–7，Text 1）、简答题（8–14，Text 2，NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER，从原文抄词，拼错算错）。

  5. 点“检查答案”会显示得分和用时。错题写错因：FALSE 和 NOT GIVEN 混了 / 同义替换没认出 / 超字数或拼错 / 时间不够。做完再点“显示全文翻译”对照，挑 10 个虚线词记到底部。

Text 1: Lakeside Council: your guide to household waste and recycling

Read the text below and answer Questions 1–7.

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落字母（A–I）可单独切换该段翻译

A Every household in Lakeside has three bins. The red-lid bin is for general waste and is collected every week. The yellow-lid bin is for recycling and the green-lid bin is for garden waste; both of these are collected fortnightly每两周一次, on alternate weeks隔周轮换, so you will have one of them emptied each week together with your red bin. Please place your bins at the kerb路边，人行道边 by 6 am on collection day, with the lids fully closed and at least 50 cm between each bin.

【A 段译文】Lakeside 每户人家都有三个垃圾桶。红盖桶装普通垃圾，每周收一次。黄盖桶装可回收物，绿盖桶装园林垃圾；这两个桶都是两周收一次，而且隔周轮换，所以每周除了红桶，还会收其中一个。请在收垃圾当天早上 6 点前把垃圾桶放到路边，盖子要盖严，每个桶之间至少隔 50 厘米。

B You can put paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans and hard plastic containers in your yellow bin. Containers should be given a quick rinse冲洗, but there is no need to remove labels. Never put soft plastics软塑料, such as plastic bags and food wrap, in the yellow bin, as they get caught in the sorting machines分拣机器. Instead, take them to the collection points found at most large supermarkets.

【B 段译文】黄桶里可以放纸、纸板、玻璃瓶和玻璃罐、金属罐和硬塑料容器。容器要简单冲洗一下，但不用撕掉标签。千万不要把软塑料，比如塑料袋和保鲜膜，放进黄桶，因为它们会卡住分拣机器。请把它们拿到大多数大型超市都有的回收点。

C Each household is entitled to有权享有 two free bulky waste大件垃圾 collections per year. These must be booked by phone or online at least one week in advance. You may put out up to two cubic metres立方米 of items, such as furniture, mattresses床垫 and fridges. Building materials such as bricks and tiles, as well as car tyres轮胎, will not be accepted.

【C 段译文】每户人家每年可以享受两次免费的大件垃圾上门回收。必须至少提前一周通过电话或网上预约。最多可以放出两立方米的物品，比如家具、床垫和冰箱。砖头、瓷砖这类建筑材料，还有汽车轮胎，一律不收。

D Hazardous危险的，有害的 items, including paint, batteries and gas bottles, must never be placed in any of your bins. They can be dropped off送到，放下 at the Community Recycling Centre on Ferry Road, which is open from Thursday to Sunday, 8 am to 4 pm. The service is free for Lakeside residents, but you will need to show proof of address住址证明, such as a driver's licence or a council rates notice地方税通知单. If your bin was not emptied, please report the missed collection within 48 hours.

【D 段译文】危险物品，包括油漆、电池和煤气罐，绝对不能放进任何垃圾桶。可以把它们送到 Ferry Road 的社区回收中心，开放时间是周四到周日，早 8 点到下午 4 点。Lakeside 居民免费使用，但需要出示住址证明，比如驾照或市政府的地方税通知单。如果你的垃圾桶没被收，请在 48 小时内报告漏收。

Text 2: Evening and weekend short courses at Westmead Community College

E Barista Skills. Learn to make espresso, flat whites and cappuccinos like a professional. Two Saturday mornings, 9 am to 12 pm. The fee of $145 includes all coffee and milk, and you will receive a certificate on completion完成时，结课时. Classes are limited to eight students so that everyone gets plenty of time on the machines. Please wear closed-toe shoes包头鞋.

【E 段译文】咖啡师技能。像专业人士一样学做浓缩咖啡、澳白和卡布奇诺。两个周六上午，9 点到 12 点。145 澳元的学费包含所有咖啡豆和牛奶，结课后会发证书。每班最多八名学员，保证每个人都有足够时间上机练习。请穿包头鞋。

F Conversational Japanese for Beginners. Eight Tuesday evenings, 6.30 pm to 8.30 pm. Course fee $220; the textbook is not included and costs an extra $35. Taught by a native speaker, this course focuses on everyday situations such as shopping and ordering food. No prior knowledge事先掌握的知识，基础 of the language is required.

【F 段译文】零基础日语会话。八个周二晚上，6 点半到 8 点半。学费 220 澳元；教材不包含在内，另付 35 澳元。由母语老师授课，重点学购物、点餐这类日常场景。不需要任何日语基础。

G First Aid Certificate. One full Sunday, 8.30 am to 5 pm. Before the course day, you must complete an online module（课程）单元, which takes about three hours. Fee $120. The certificate is valid for three years and is a requirement for many jobs in childcare, aged care and hospitality.

【G 段译文】急救证书。一整个周日，早 8 点半到下午 5 点。上课那天之前，必须先完成一个网上学习单元，大约需要三小时。学费 120 澳元。证书有效期三年，很多托儿、养老和餐饮酒店类工作都要求有这个证书。

H Introduction to Bookkeeping簿记，记账. Six Wednesday evenings, 6 pm to 9 pm. Fee $260. Ideal for small business owners who want to keep their own accounts. Students must bring a laptop with spreadsheet电子表格 software already installed.

【H 段译文】簿记入门。六个周三晚上，6 点到 9 点。学费 260 澳元。很适合想自己记账的小企业主。学员必须带一台已经装好电子表格软件的笔记本电脑。

I How to enrol: you can enrol online or at reception, which is open on weekdays from 9 am to 5 pm. Full payment is required at the time of enrolment. Students, pensioners领养老金的人 and job seekers with a valid concession card优惠卡 receive a 20% discount. If you cancel more than seven days before your course starts, you will get a full refund退款 minus a $15 administration fee. Later cancellations are not refunded, but you may transfer转（课程） to another course. If a course is cancelled because not enough people have enrolled, you will receive a full refund.

【I 段译文】报名方式：可以网上报名，也可以去前台报名，前台工作日早 9 点到下午 5 点开放。报名时必须付清全款。持有效优惠卡的学生、领养老金的老人和求职者可以打八折。如果在开课前七天以上取消，可以全额退款，但要扣 15 澳元手续费。更晚取消不退款，但可以转到别的课程。如果因为报名人数不够而取消课程，会全额退款。

Questions 1–7

Do the following statements agree with the information given in Text 1? Choose TRUE if the statement agrees with the information, FALSE if the statement contradicts the information, NOT GIVEN if there is no information on this.

  1. The recycling bin and the garden waste bin are emptied in the same week.
  2. Residents may be fined if they put their bins out too early.
  3. Labels must be taken off containers before they are put in the yellow bin.
  4. Many large supermarkets accept plastic bags for recycling.
  5. A third bulky waste collection in the same year is available for a fee.
  6. Old car tyres can be left out for a bulky waste collection.
  7. Residents need to prove where they live to use the Community Recycling Centre for free.

Questions 8–14

Answer the questions below. Choose NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER from Text 2 for each answer.

  1. What kind of footwear must students on the barista course wear?
  2. How much does the Japanese textbook cost?
  3. What must First Aid students finish before the course day?
  4. How long is the First Aid certificate valid for?
  5. What piece of equipment do bookkeeping students need to bring?
  6. What discount do people with a concession card get?
  7. How much is the administration fee taken from an early cancellation refund?

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. FALSE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…both of these are collected fortnightly, on alternate weeks…

alternate weeks 是隔周轮换，黄桶和绿桶不在同一周收，所以 FALSE。

2. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Please place your bins at the kerb by 6 am on collection day…

原文只说 6 点前放好，没提放早了会不会罚款，NOT GIVEN。别用常识脑补。

3. FALSE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…there is no need to remove labels.

must be taken off 和 no need to remove 正好相反，FALSE。

4. TRUE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…take them to the collection points found at most large supermarkets.

them 指 soft plastics，包括 plastic bags；many = most，TRUE。

5. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Each household is entitled to two free bulky waste collections per year.

只说每年两次免费，第三次能不能付费预约没说，NOT GIVEN。

6. FALSE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…as well as car tyres, will not be accepted.

tyres 明确不收，FALSE。

7. TRUE（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：The service is free for Lakeside residents, but you will need to show proof of address…

prove where they live = show proof of address，TRUE。

8. closed-toe shoes / closed toe shoes / closed-toe（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Please wear closed-toe shoes.

footwear = shoes，填 closed-toe shoes，三个词以内。

9. $35 / 35（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…the textbook is not included and costs an extra $35.

填 $35（写 35 也对）。别填 220，那是学费。

10. online module / an online module（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：Before the course day, you must complete an online module…

finish = complete，填 online module。

11. three years / 3 years（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：The certificate is valid for three years…

填 three years 或 3 years。注意别和 three hours 弄混。

12. laptop / a laptop（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：Students must bring a laptop with spreadsheet software already installed.

piece of equipment = laptop，填 laptop。

13. 20% / 20 percent / 20 per cent（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：…with a valid concession card receive a 20% discount.

填 20%。

14. $15 / 15（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：…a full refund minus a $15 administration fee.

taken from = minus，填 $15（写 15 也对）。
