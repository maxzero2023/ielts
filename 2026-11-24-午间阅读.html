Text 1: Weekend markets in Hillcrest

Look at the five advertisements, A–E.

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落字母（A–I）可单独切换该段翻译

A Riverside Farmers' Market. Every Saturday from 7 am to noon in the car park of Riverside Primary School. Over 40 local growers种植户 sell fruit, vegetables, eggs and honey, all produced生产，出产 within 100 km of the town. Please bring your own bags, as plastic bags are not available. Free parking is available in the side streets.

【A 段译文】Riverside 农夫市集。每周六早上 7 点到中午，在 Riverside 小学的停车场举行。40 多家本地种植户在这里卖水果、蔬菜、鸡蛋和蜂蜜，全部产自镇子 100 公里以内。请自带购物袋，这里不提供塑料袋。附近小街可以免费停车。

B Old Mill Antiques & Collectables Fair. First Sunday of every month, 9 am to 4 pm, inside the historic Old Mill building, so the fair goes ahead照常进行 whatever the weather不管天气如何. Entry is $3 for adults; children under 12 are free. Experienced dealers商贩 will value估价 your old items for a small fee.

【B 段译文】Old Mill 古董与收藏品集市。每月第一个周日，早 9 点到下午 4 点，在有历史的 Old Mill 老磨坊建筑里面举行，所以不管天气怎样都照常开。成人门票 3 澳元，12 岁以下儿童免费。经验丰富的商贩可以帮你给旧物估价，收一点小费用。

C Harbour Night Market. Friday evenings from 5 pm to 10 pm, November to March only. More than 30 food stalls小吃摊 offer dishes from around the world, and local bands perform live music on the main stage. Dogs are welcome as long as they are kept on a lead拴着牵引绳.

【C 段译文】Harbour 夜市。周五晚上 5 点到 10 点，只在 11 月到次年 3 月开。30 多个小吃摊提供世界各地的菜，本地乐队在主舞台现场演出。欢迎带狗，只要拴着牵引绳就行。

D Hillcrest Makers' Market. Every second Saturday, 10 am to 3 pm, in the Town Hall gardens. Everything on sale is handmade手工制作的 by the stallholders摊主 themselves, including jewellery, pottery陶器, soaps and children's clothing. Interested in selling? Stall sites cost $40 per day and must be booked online at least two weeks ahead.

【D 段译文】Hillcrest 手作市集。每隔一周的周六，上午 10 点到下午 3 点，在市政厅花园举行。所有商品都是摊主自己手工做的，包括首饰、陶器、香皂和童装。想来摆摊？摊位每天 40 澳元，必须至少提前两周在网上预订。

E Southside Car Boot Sale. Every Sunday from 6 am in the Southside Sports Ground car park. Turn up with a car full of unwanted household goods不要的家居用品 and sell straight from your boot（汽车）后备箱. It costs $10 per car, with no booking needed: just pay at the gate. Buyers enter free. Cash only, as there is no ATM on site.

【E 段译文】Southside 后备箱集市。每周日早上 6 点起，在 Southside 体育场停车场举行。开一车不要的家居用品过来，直接从后备箱卖。每辆车 10 澳元，不用预约，在门口交钱就行。买家免费入场。只收现金，现场没有取款机。

Text 2: Starting a tenancy: bonds, condition reports and repairs

F Before you move in, most landlords will ask you to pay a rental bond租房押金. This is a sum of money held as security担保，抵押 in case you damage the property or fail to pay the rent. The bond is usually equal to four weeks' rent. By law, your landlord or agent must lodge提交，交存 the bond with the Rental Bond Board within 10 working days工作日 of receiving it; it must not be kept in the landlord's own bank account. You will then be sent a receipt showing your bond number, which you should keep in a safe place.

【F 段译文】搬进去之前，大多数房东会要求你交一笔租房押金。这笔钱作为担保，以防你损坏房子或者不交房租。押金通常等于四周的房租。按照法律，房东或中介必须在收到押金后 10 个工作日内把它交给租房押金管理局保管，不能放在房东自己的银行账户里。之后你会收到一张写着押金编号的收据，要放在安全的地方。

G On or before the day you move in, the agent must give you two copies of a condition report房屋状况报告. This document describes the state of each room, including walls, floors, windows and appliances家用电器. Go through the property carefully and write down anything that is missing, broken or dirty. It is also a good idea to take dated photographs. You have seven days to return one signed copy to the agent; keep the other for your own records.

【G 段译文】在你搬进去当天或之前，中介必须给你两份房屋状况报告。这份文件描述每个房间的状况，包括墙、地板、窗户和家电。仔细检查一遍房子，把缺的、坏的、脏的东西都写下来。最好也拍一些带日期的照片。你有七天时间把一份签好字的报告交回中介，另一份自己留底。

H During the tenancy, you are responsible for keeping the property reasonably clean and for reporting any repairs promptly及时地. Urgent repairs, such as a burst爆裂的 water pipe, a gas leak or a broken toilet, should be reported immediately by phone. If you cannot reach the landlord or agent, you may arrange for a qualified tradesperson技工 to fix the problem, up to a cost of $1,000, and then ask to be paid back. Non-urgent repairs should always be requested in writing.

【H 段译文】租住期间，你有责任让房子保持基本干净，并且及时报修。紧急维修，比如水管爆裂、燃气泄漏或者马桶坏了，要马上打电话报告。如果联系不上房东或中介，你可以自己找有资质的技工来修，费用最多 1000 澳元，之后再要求对方报销。非紧急的维修一律要书面申请。

I When you move out, the agent will inspect the property and compare it with the original condition report. Normal wear and tear正常磨损, such as faded curtains or small marks on the walls caused by furniture, cannot be deducted扣除 from your bond. If the property is in the same condition as when you moved in, you and the agent both sign a claim form, and the full bond is returned to your nominated指定的 bank account, usually within a few days.

【I 段译文】你搬走的时候，中介会检查房子，并和最初的房屋状况报告对比。正常磨损，比如窗帘褪色，或者家具在墙上蹭出的小印子，不能从押金里扣。如果房子和你搬进来时状况一样，你和中介一起签一张申领表，全部押金会退到你指定的银行账户，一般几天内到账。
