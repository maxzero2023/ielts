做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN 判断（1–7）和简答题（8–14）。
  2. 判断题口诀：原文意思一致选 TRUE，原文说的跟题目相反选 FALSE，原文压根没说选 NOT GIVEN。别用常识补脑，只看原文。
  3. FALSE 和 NOT GIVEN 最容易混：原文有一句话能直接推翻题目才是 FALSE；找不到能比较的信息就是 NOT GIVEN。今天第 5、7 题专门练这个。
  4. 8–14 字数限制是 NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER，最多两个词加一个数字，超字数算错。答案按题目顺序出现在 Text 2 里。

Text 1: Coastline Ferries, Information for Passengers

A Booking and tickets
Tickets can be bought online, by phone or at the harbour office. Online bookings are £3 cheaper than tickets bought at the office on the day of travel. Return tickets are valid有效的 for one month from the date of the outward journey去程. Children under five travel free, but they must still have a ticket, which can be collected领取 from the office.

【A 段译文】船票可以在网上、通过电话或在港口售票处购买。网上预订比出发当天在售票处买的票便宜3英镑。往返票自去程日期起一个月内有效。五岁以下儿童免费乘船，但仍必须有一张票，可在售票处领取。

B Check-in
Foot passengers步行乘客（不开车上船的） should check in at least 30 minutes before departure出发；开船. Drivers must arrive no later than不晚于 45 minutes before the ship leaves, as vehicles are loaded装载 first. Check-in closes strictly严格地 at these times, and late arrivals may be moved to the next available sailing（船的）一个班次.

【B 段译文】步行乘客应至少在开船前30分钟办理登船手续。司机必须在开船前45分钟之前到达，因为车辆先装船。登船手续严格按上述时间截止，迟到的乘客可能会被改到下一班有空位的船。

C Luggage and pets
Each foot passenger may bring two pieces of luggage free of charge免费. Bicycles can be carried for a small fee, but they must be booked in advance提前 as space is limited. Dogs are welcome on board but must stay on a lead拴着狗绳 in the outside deck甲板 areas. They are not allowed in the café or the lounge休息室, except for assistance dogs辅助犬（如导盲犬）.

【C 段译文】每位步行乘客可免费携带两件行李。自行车可以付少量费用随船携带，但由于空间有限，必须提前预订。欢迎狗上船，但在室外甲板区域必须拴着狗绳。狗不能进入咖啡厅或休息室，导盲犬等辅助犬除外。

D Bad weather
Sailings are occasionally偶尔 cancelled when winds are very strong. If your sailing is cancelled, you can either travel on the next available ship at no extra cost or claim a full refund申请全额退款. Updates are posted on our website and sent by text message to passengers who gave a mobile number when booking.

【D 段译文】风很大时，航班偶尔会被取消。如果您的航班被取消，您可以免费改乘下一班有空位的船，也可以申请全额退款。最新消息会发布在我们的网站上，并通过短信发给预订时留了手机号的乘客。

Text 2: Ashford Farmers' Market, A Guide for Visitors

E When and where
Ashford Farmers' Market takes place on the first and third Saturday of every month in the car park behind the town hall. Stalls摊位 open at 8.30 am and close at 1 pm. Free parking is available at the leisure centre on Park Lane, a two-minute walk away.

【E 段译文】Ashford 农夫市集每月第一个和第三个周六在市政厅后面的停车场举办。摊位上午8:30开门，下午1点收摊。Park Lane 上的休闲中心提供免费停车，步行两分钟即到。

F What you can buy
All stallholders摊主 must grow, rear饲养 or make what they sell within 30 miles of Ashford. You will find fresh vegetables, meat, cheese, bread and honey, as well as plants and flowers in spring and summer. Many stalls accept cards, but there is a cash machine取款机 inside the town hall for those who need one.

【F 段译文】所有摊主卖的东西都必须是在 Ashford 方圆30英里内自己种的、养的或做的。这里有新鲜蔬菜、肉、奶酪、面包和蜂蜜，春夏两季还有植物和鲜花。很多摊位可以刷卡，但市政厅里面也有一台取款机，供需要现金的人使用。

G Tips for visitors
Arrive early for the best choice, as popular items such as eggs often sell out卖光 by ten o'clock. Please bring your own shopping bags; paper bags can be bought for 10p each. A tasting tent near the entrance offers free samples试吃样品 every week, and a local cook gives a cooking demonstration示范；演示 at 11 am on the third Saturday.

【G 段译文】早点来可以挑到最好的，因为鸡蛋这类热门商品常常十点前就卖光了。请自带购物袋；纸袋每个10便士。入口附近的试吃帐篷每周都提供免费试吃，每月第三个周六上午11点还有一位本地厨师做烹饪示范。

Questions 1–7

Do the following statements agree with the information given in Text 1? Choose TRUE if the statement agrees with the information, FALSE if the statement contradicts the information, NOT GIVEN if there is no information on this.

  1. Buying a ticket online costs less than buying one at the harbour office on the day of travel.
  2. Return tickets must be used within two weeks of the outward journey.
  3. Children under five do not need a ticket.
  4. Drivers have to check in earlier than foot passengers.
  5. Passengers who arrive after check-in closes will get their money back.
  6. Bicycles are carried free of charge.
  7. Sailings are cancelled more often in winter than in summer.

Questions 8–14

Answer the questions below. Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER from Text 2 for each answer.

  1. Where exactly is the market held?
  2. At what time do the stalls close?
  3. On which road can visitors park for free?
  4. Within what distance of Ashford must sellers produce their goods?
  5. In which building can shoppers get cash?
  6. Which popular item often sells out early?
  7. How much does one paper bag cost?

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. TRUE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Online bookings are £3 cheaper than tickets bought at the office on the day of travel.

costs less = £3 cheaper，TRUE。

2. FALSE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Return tickets are valid for one month from the date of the outward journey.

原文是一个月，题目说两周，矛盾，FALSE。

3. FALSE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Children under five travel free, but they must still have a ticket…

免费不等于不用票，原文说 must still have a ticket，FALSE。

4. TRUE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：Foot passengers … at least 30 minutes … Drivers … no later than 45 minutes before…

司机要提前45分钟，步行30分钟，司机更早，TRUE。

5. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…late arrivals may be moved to the next available sailing.

原文只说可能改到下一班，没说迟到能退款，NOT GIVEN。退款出现在 D 段，那是取消航班的情况，别串。

6. FALSE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Bicycles can be carried for a small fee…

for a small fee 是要收钱，和 free of charge 矛盾，FALSE。免费的是两件行李。

7. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：Sailings are occasionally cancelled when winds are very strong.

只说风大时偶尔取消，没比较冬天和夏天，NOT GIVEN。

8. car park / the car park / car park behind the town hall（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…in the car park behind the town hall.

填 car park。town hall 是地标，题问举办地点本身。

9. 1 pm / 1pm / 1（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Stalls open at 8.30 am and close at 1 pm.

close 对应 1 pm，别填开门时间 8.30。

10. park lane（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Free parking is available at the leisure centre on Park Lane…

on which road 问路名，填 Park Lane。

11. 30 miles / thirty miles（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…grow, rear or make what they sell within 30 miles of Ashford.

produce their goods = grow, rear or make，填 30 miles。

12. town hall / the town hall（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…there is a cash machine inside the town hall…

get cash = cash machine，填 town hall。

13. eggs（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…popular items such as eggs often sell out by ten o'clock.

sells out early = sell out by ten o'clock，填 eggs。

14. 10p / 10 p / 10 pence（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…paper bags can be bought for 10p each.

填 10p。注意 10p 是10便士，不是10英镑。
