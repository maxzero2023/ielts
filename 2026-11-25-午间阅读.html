Text 1: Lifting and carrying safely: guidance for warehouse and retail staff

Read the text below and answer Questions 1–7.

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落字母（A–I）可单独切换该段翻译

A Every year, manual handling injuries account for占（比例） about a third of all workplace injury claims in the retail and warehouse sectors. Most of these are back and shoulder strains拉伤, and they are rarely caused by a single heavy lift. More often, damage builds up gradually逐渐地 through repeated awkward别扭的，不顺手的 movements, such as twisting while holding a box or reaching above head height. This guide explains what the company expects of you and what you can expect from us.

【A 段译文】每年，在零售和仓储行业的所有工伤索赔里，搬运类受伤大约占三分之一。其中大多数是背部和肩部拉伤，而且很少是一次搬重物造成的。更常见的是，伤害通过反复的别扭动作慢慢积累起来，比如抱着箱子扭身，或者把手伸到头顶以上去够东西。这份指南说明公司对你有什么要求，以及你可以从我们这里得到什么。

B Before lifting anything, assess评估 the load（要搬的）货物，负重. Check its weight, which is often printed on the side of the packaging, and look for sharp edges or leaking渗漏的 contents. If an item weighs more than 16 kg, you must not lift it alone: ask a colleague to help or use one of the trolleys or pallet jacks托盘搬运车 provided in every storeroom. Plan your route too, and make sure the path is clear of obstacles障碍物 before you start.

【B 段译文】搬任何东西之前，先评估一下货物。看看它有多重，重量常常印在包装侧面，再检查有没有锋利的边角或者漏出来的东西。如果一件货超过 16 公斤，你绝对不能一个人搬：请同事帮忙，或者用每个库房都配备的手推车或托盘搬运车。也要提前规划路线，开始之前确认路上没有障碍物。

C When you lift, stand close to the load with your feet shoulder-width与肩同宽 apart. Bend your knees rather than your back, get a firm grip握，抓牢, and keep the item close to your body as you stand up. Avoid twisting; turn by moving your feet instead. Put the load down in the same careful way. Staff who regularly work on high shelving货架 must use the step platforms supplied, never chairs or boxes.

【C 段译文】搬的时候，站得离货物近一点，双脚与肩同宽。弯膝盖而不是弯腰，抓牢，站起来的时候让东西贴近身体。避免扭身，要转向就挪动双脚。放下货物时也要同样小心。经常在高货架上工作的员工必须使用公司配的登高踏台，绝不能用椅子或箱子垫脚。

D All new employees attend a 45-minute manual handling session during their first week. The session is run by our health and safety officer and includes practical demonstrations示范. Refresher training复训，进修培训 is offered every two years, although team leaders may ask staff to repeat it sooner if they notice unsafe habits.

【D 段译文】所有新员工入职第一周都要参加一次 45 分钟的搬运安全培训。培训由我们的健康与安全专员主持，包括实际操作示范。复训每两年提供一次，不过组长如果发现员工有不安全的习惯，可以要求他们提前重新参加。

E If you feel pain or discomfort不适 during or after a task, stop and tell your supervisor straight away, even if it seems minor. Every injury, however small, must be recorded in the incident book事故登记簿 kept at the staff entrance within 24 hours. Early reporting means problems can be dealt with before they become serious, and it also protects your right to compensation赔偿 if you need time off work.

【E 段译文】如果你在干活时或干完后感到疼痛或不适，要停下来马上告诉主管，哪怕看起来是小问题。每一次受伤，不管多小，都必须在 24 小时内登记在员工入口处的事故登记簿上。尽早报告意味着问题能在变严重之前得到处理，而且万一你需要请假休养，这也能保护你获得赔偿的权利。

Text 2: Understanding your pay: payslips, tax and superannuation

F When you start a new job in Australia, your employer will ask you to complete a Tax File Number (TFN) declaration. You do not have to provide your TFN, but if you don't, tax will be taken from your pay at the highest rate. Your employer must give you a payslip within one working day of paying you, either on paper or by email.

【F 段译文】在澳大利亚开始一份新工作时，雇主会让你填写一份税号（TFN）申报表。你不是必须提供税号，但如果不提供，你的工资会按最高税率扣税。雇主必须在给你发工资后的一个工作日内给你工资单，纸质的或者用电子邮件发都可以。

G Your payslip shows your gross pay税前工资, which is the total amount you earned before anything is taken out, and your net pay实发工资, the amount actually paid into your bank account. It also lists the hours you worked, your hourly rate and any extra payments, such as overtime or allowances for working at night or on public holidays.

【G 段译文】工资单上会写你的税前工资，也就是扣除任何款项之前你挣的总额，还有实发工资，也就是真正打进你银行账户的钱。上面还列出你的工作小时数、时薪，以及各种额外收入，比如加班费，或者夜班、公共假日上班的补贴。

H In addition to your wages, your employer must pay superannuation（澳洲）养老金, a form of retirement savings, into a fund of your choice. The current rate is 12% of your ordinary earnings正常收入, and these payments should appear on your payslip. If you do not choose a fund, your employer will normally use the fund you held in your previous job; only if you have never had one will they open an account for you with their default fund默认基金.

【H 段译文】除了工资，雇主还必须把养老金（一种退休储蓄）存进你选的基金。目前的比例是你正常收入的 12%，这些款项应该显示在工资单上。如果你不选基金，雇主一般会用你上一份工作时的那个基金；只有你从来没有过基金账户时，他们才会在公司的默认基金给你开户。

I Check every payslip carefully. Mistakes are not unusual, especially when you change roles or your hours vary from week to week. If something looks wrong, speak to the payroll team first, as most errors are simply corrected in your next pay. If the problem is not resolved解决, you can contact the Fair Work Ombudsman监察专员, which provides free advice on pay and working conditions.

【I 段译文】每张工资单都要仔细核对。出错并不少见，尤其是你换岗位或者每周工时不固定的时候。如果哪里看着不对，先找工资核算组，因为大多数错误在下一次发工资时就会直接改过来。如果问题没解决，你可以联系公平工作监察专员署，它免费提供关于工资和工作条件的咨询。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. FALSE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…they are rarely caused by a single heavy lift.

题目说大多数伤是一次搬很重的东西造成的，原文说 rarely（很少），意思相反，选 FALSE。

2. TRUE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…use one of the trolleys or pallet jacks provided in every storeroom.

each storeroom = every storeroom，available = provided，选 TRUE。

3. TRUE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Avoid twisting; turn by moving your feet instead.

change direction = turn，选 TRUE。

4. FALSE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…must use the step platforms supplied, never chairs or boxes.

原文说 never chairs，题目说可以站椅子，直接矛盾，选 FALSE。if no step platform is nearby 是干扰条件。

5. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：The session is run by our health and safety officer and includes practical demonstrations.

原文只说有操作示范，完全没提考试，选 NOT GIVEN。别因为常识觉得培训都要考试。

6. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：Refresher training is offered every two years…

原文只说复训每两年提供一次，没说员工自己选择多久参加一次，也没说大多数人每年去，选 NOT GIVEN。

7. FALSE（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Every injury, however small, must be recorded in the incident book…

however small = 再小也要登记，题目说小伤不用登记，选 FALSE。

8. declaration（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…complete a Tax File Number (TFN) declaration.

fill in = complete，填 declaration。

9. highest（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…tax will be taken from your pay at the highest rate.

taxed = tax will be taken，填 highest。

10. email / e-mail（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…either on paper or by email.

原词题，填 email（e-mail 也算对）。

11. gross（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…your gross pay, which is the total amount you earned before anything is taken out…

before any money is taken out = before anything is taken out，填 gross。空格后已有 pay，别填 gross pay。

12. allowances（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…such as overtime or allowances for working at night or on public holidays.

填 allowances，注意复数，写 allowance 算错。

13. retirement（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…superannuation, a form of retirement savings…

a type of = a form of，填 retirement。

14. payroll（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：…speak to the payroll team first, as most errors are simply corrected in your next pay.

mistakes = errors，sorted out = corrected，填 payroll。
