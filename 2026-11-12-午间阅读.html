Text 1：Hartley Logistics: Flexible Working Options

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号可单独切换该段翻译

Intro At Hartley Logistics, we understand that employees sometimes need to change the way they work, for example because of childcare or caring for an elderly relative年迈的亲属. All staff have the right to request flexible working弹性工作. The five main options are described below.

【Intro 段译文】在哈特利物流公司，我们理解员工有时需要改变工作方式，比如因为要照顾孩子或照顾年迈的亲属。所有员工都有权申请弹性工作。下面介绍五种主要的方式。

A Compressed hours
Staff work their usual total of 37.5 hours over four days instead of five, giving them one extra day off each week. Days are therefore longer, usually from 8 am to 6 pm. This option is not available to warehouse staff on night shifts夜班, as their shifts are already ten hours long. The day off must be agreed with your team leader组长 and normally stays the same each week.

【A 段译文】员工把平时总共37.5小时的工作量压缩到四天而不是五天完成，这样每周就多出一天休息。因此每天的工作时间更长，通常是早上8点到晚上6点。上夜班的仓库员工不能选这种方式，因为他们的班次本来就有十个小时。休息日必须和你的组长商定，通常每周固定不变。

B Part-time working
You reduce the number of hours you work, and your salary and holiday entitlement应得的权利（这里指带薪假） are reduced in proportion按比例. For example, an employee working three days a week receives 60% of full-time pay. Part-time staff keep all other benefits, including the staff discount and the company pension养老金. Many parents choose this option while their children are young. You can ask to return to full-time hours later, although this cannot be guaranteed保证的.

【B 段译文】你减少工作时数，工资和带薪假期也按比例减少。比如，每周工作三天的员工拿全职工资的60%。兼职员工保留其他所有福利，包括员工折扣和公司养老金。很多家长在孩子还小的时候选择这种方式。之后你可以申请恢复全职，但不能保证一定批准。

C Job sharing
Two employees share the responsibilities职责 of one full-time position, each working around half of the hours. Job sharers must arrange a handover工作交接, usually a short meeting or phone call, when one person finishes and the other begins. If you are interested but do not have a partner in mind, the HR department keeps a list of colleagues who are looking for someone to share with.

【C 段译文】两名员工分担一个全职岗位的职责，各自工作大约一半的时间。一人下班、另一人接班时，岗位共享者必须安排交接，通常是一个简短的会议或电话。如果你感兴趣但没有合适的搭档人选，人事部有一份正在寻找共享搭档的同事名单。

D Working from home
Office-based staff may work from home for up to two days a week. You will need a quiet space and a reliable可靠的；稳定的 internet connection. The company will provide a laptop and headset头戴式耳机, but not a desk or chair. Your manager may ask you to come into the office on a home-working day if there is an important meeting. This option is only offered to staff who have worked for the company for at least six months.

【D 段译文】坐办公室的员工每周最多可以在家工作两天。你需要一个安静的空间和稳定的网络连接。公司会提供笔记本电脑和耳机，但不提供桌椅。如果有重要会议，经理可能会让你在居家办公日到办公室来。这种方式只提供给在公司工作满六个月的员工。

E Staggered hours
Your working day stays the same length, but you start and finish at different times from the standard 9 am to 5.30 pm. For example, you might work from 7 am to 3.30 pm to avoid the rush hour上下班高峰期 or to collect children from school. Everyone must be present during the 'core hours核心时段' of 10 am to 3 pm. This is the simplest option to arrange and is usually approved within a week.

【E 段译文】你每天的工作时长不变，但上下班时间和标准的上午9点到下午5点半不同。比如你可以早上7点工作到下午3点半，以避开高峰期或去学校接孩子。所有人在上午10点到下午3点的“核心时段”必须在岗。这是最容易安排的一种方式，通常一周内就能批下来。

F Making a request
Requests should be made in writing using form FW1, which is available on the staff intranet内网. Explain which option you would like, when you would like it to start and how you think it might affect your team. Your manager must respond within one month. If your request is refused, you can appeal申诉 once within 14 days.

【F 段译文】申请须用 FW1 表格书面提交，表格可以在员工内网上找到。说明你想选哪种方式、希望什么时候开始，以及你认为这会对团队有什么影响。经理必须在一个月内答复。如果申请被拒，你可以在14天内申诉一次。

Text 2：Become a Workplace First Aider

G Hartley Logistics needs at least one trained first aider for every 50 employees on each site. First aiders are the people who give immediate即时的 help when a colleague is injured受伤的 or suddenly becomes ill, until an ambulance or doctor arrives. Any permanent employee can volunteer, but you should be calm under pressure压力下保持冷静 and able to kneel跪 on the floor for several minutes.

【G 段译文】哈特利物流公司每个厂区每50名员工至少需要一名受过培训的急救员。急救员就是在同事受伤或突然生病时，在救护车或医生到达之前提供即时帮助的人。任何正式员工都可以报名，但你应该能在压力下保持冷静，并且能在地上跪几分钟。

H The training course lasts three days and takes place at the regional training centre in Milton. It is run by a qualified paramedic急救医护人员 and covers how to deal with burns, broken bones and heart attacks. On the final afternoon there is a practical assessment考核, and those who pass receive a certificate that is valid for three years. Before it expires到期, first aiders must attend a one-day refresher course复训课程 to keep their qualification.

【H 段译文】培训课程为期三天，在米尔顿的地区培训中心进行。课程由一名持证急救医护人员主讲，内容包括如何处理烧伤、骨折和心脏病发作。最后一天下午有实操考核，通过的人会拿到有效期三年的证书。证书到期前，急救员必须参加为期一天的复训课程，才能保留资格。

I The course is free, and you will be paid your normal wage while you attend. In recognition of为了表彰/认可 the extra responsibility, first aiders also receive an additional payment of £25 per month. Your name and photograph will be displayed on the notice board next to the staff canteen so that colleagues know who to contact. If you would like to take part, speak to the site's health and safety officer, Ms Patel, who arranges places on each course.

【I 段译文】课程免费，参加培训期间照发正常工资。为了表彰这份额外的责任，急救员每月还能额外拿到25英镑的补贴。你的名字和照片会贴在员工食堂旁边的公告栏上，让同事知道该找谁。如果你想参加，请找本厂区的健康与安全主管帕特尔女士，她负责安排每期课程的名额。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Everyone must be present during the 'core hours' of 10 am to 3 pm.

must be at work = must be present，a fixed part of the day = core hours，选 E。

2. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…your salary and holiday entitlement are reduced in proportion.

in proportion = by the same percentage，按比例减工资，选 B。

3. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：The company will provide a laptop and headset, but not a desk or chair.

supplies some of the equipment = provide a laptop and headset（只给一部分，所以是 some），选 D。
