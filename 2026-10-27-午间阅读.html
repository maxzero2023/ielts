做题要求
  1. Section 2 是工作场景的文章，比 Section 1 长一点，词汇偏职场（entitled to、supervisor、in advance）。今天两种新题型：段落标题匹配（1–7）和笔记填空（8–14）。
  2. 标题匹配：先读完每段再看标题，抓整段大意，不要因为某个单词一样就选。标题有 10 个，段落只有 7 段，有 3 个是干扰项。
  3. 笔记填空：NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS，每空最多两个词，必须是原文里的词，拼写要对。题目按原文顺序，先找空格前后的同义词再定位。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：只看单词没看段意 / 同义替换没认出 / 拼写或单复数错 / 超字数。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Brightwater Hotel: Information for New Staff

A All new employees are given two sets of uniform on their first day. These must be kept clean and pressed熨平的 at all times, and the hotel laundry will wash them free of charge免费 if they are left in the basket by the staff entrance before 10 am. Jewellery should be kept to a minimum尽量少, and staff in the kitchen must not wear rings or watches for hygiene卫生 reasons.

B Each member of staff is assigned分配 a locker in the basement changing room. You will need to provide your own padlock挂锁. The hotel cannot accept responsibility承担责任 for valuables贵重物品 that are lost or stolen, so we strongly advise you not to bring large amounts of cash or expensive items to work.

C During your first two weeks, you will work alongside和……一起 a ‘buddy’, an experienced member of your team who can answer your questions and show you how things are done. At the end of this period, your supervisor主管 will meet you to discuss your progress and identify any further training you may need.

D Full-time staff are entitled to有权享有 28 days of paid holiday a year, including public holidays. Requests should be made through the online staff portal at least four weeks in advance提前. Please note that leave is rarely approved批准 during the busy period from mid-December to early January, so plan accordingly.

E If you are too ill to come in, you must phone your supervisor at least two hours before your shift is due to start. Sending a text message or email is not acceptable. For absences of more than seven days in a row连续, you will be asked to provide a note from your doctor.

F Staff working a shift of six hours or more are entitled to a hot meal in the staff canteen, which is open from 11 am to 9 pm. There is no charge for this, but you must show your staff ID card. Tea, coffee and fruit are available throughout the day.

G Personal mobile phones must be switched to silent while you are on duty上班时；值班中 and must not be used in any area where guests can see you. You may check your phone during your breaks. In an emergency, family members can contact you by calling the main reception desk.

Safe Lifting: A Guide for All Staff

1 Back injuries are one of the most common reasons for time off work in the hospitality industry酒店餐饮业. Most of them are caused not by a single accident but by repeated lifting in the wrong way over a long period. Before lifting anything, take a moment to assess评估 the load. If it is heavier than about 20 kilograms, or an awkward不好拿的；别扭的 shape, do not attempt to lift it alone. Ask a colleague for help or use one of the trolleys kept in the storeroom.

2 When you do lift, stand close to the object with your feet slightly apart, one foot a little in front of the other. Bend your knees rather than your back, and keep your back as straight as possible. Get a firm grip抓牢, then lift smoothly using the strength of your legs. Avoid twisting扭转 your body while carrying the load; instead, turn by moving your feet.

3 Keep the load close to your waist, as this reduces the strain压力；劳损 on your lower back. Make sure you can see where you are going, and check that the route is clear of obstacles障碍物 before you start. When putting the load down, follow the same steps in reverse倒过来.

4 Any injury, however minor不管多小 it seems, must be reported to the duty manager and recorded in the accident book, which is kept at the security office. All staff who regularly handle heavy items will also attend a 90-minute manual handling人工搬运 course within their first month.
