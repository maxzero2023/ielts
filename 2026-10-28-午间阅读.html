做题要求
  1. Section 3 是 G 类里最长最难的一篇，接近学术类文章，句子长、同义替换多。今天三种题型：判断题 TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN（1–6）、单选题（7–10）、摘要填空（11–14）。
  2. 判断题：和原文意思一致选 TRUE，相反选 FALSE，原文根本没说选 NOT GIVEN。最常见的错是把“没提到”当成 FALSE，或者自己脑补成 TRUE。
  3. 单选题：先读题干找关键词定位，再逐个排除。选项里出现原文原词的，往往是干扰项。
  4. 摘要填空：NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS，必须用原文的词，拼写和单复数要对。
  5. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：NG 和 F 混了 / 同义替换没认出 / 被原词干扰 / 拼写或超字数。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

The Box That Changed the World

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号（A–F）可单独切换该段翻译

A Almost everything we buy, from trainers to televisions, has spent part of its life inside a steel box. The shipping container is so ordinary that most people never notice it, yet economists often describe it as one of the most important inventions of the twentieth century. Its importance lies在于 not in any clever technology but in a simple idea: goods should be packed once, at the factory, and not handled again until they reach their destination目的地.

【A 段译文】我们购买的几乎所有东西，从运动鞋到电视机，在送达我们手中之前都曾在铁皮箱里度过一段时间。集装箱是如此寻常，以至于大多数人从来不会留意它，然而经济学家却常将其称为二十世纪最重要的发明之一。它的重要性并不在于任何巧妙的高科技，而在于一个简单的理念：货物应该在工厂里一次性装箱打包，在到达目的地之前不再被拆装搬动。

B Before containers, cargo货物 was loaded onto ships piece by piece. Sacks, barrels and wooden crates板条箱 were carried on board by teams of dock workers, a process known as break-bulk shipping. A large vessel船只 could spend a week or more in port being loaded and unloaded, and in many ports the cost of handling goods was greater than the cost of carrying them across the ocean. Theft was also widespread普遍的, and damage to fragile易碎的 goods was a constant problem.

【B 段译文】在集装箱出现之前，货物是一小件一小件装上船的。成群的码头工人把麻袋、木桶和木制板条箱扛上船，这种装运方式被称为散杂货运输。一艘大船光是在港口装货和卸货就可能要花上一周甚至更久，而且在许多港口，装卸货物的费用比把它们运过重洋的运费还要高。盗窃现象也十分普遍，易碎物品的损坏更是常有的问题。

C The man usually credited with被认为是……的功臣 changing this was Malcom McLean, an American who owned a trucking company rather than a shipping line. Frustrated受挫的；恼火的 by the hours his drivers spent waiting at the docks, he wondered why the whole trailer could not simply be lifted onto a ship. In 1956 a converted改装的 oil tanker, the Ideal X, sailed from New Jersey to Texas carrying 58 metal boxes. McLean had calculated that loading cargo this way cost only a small fraction一小部分 of the traditional method.

【C 段译文】通常被认为改变了这一切的人是马尔科姆·麦克莱恩（Malcom McLean），他是一名美国人，经营的是一家卡车货运公司，而不是航运公司。看到手下的司机在码头一等就是几个小时，他感到十分恼火，于是琢磨：为什么不能直接把整个拖车车厢吊到船上呢？1956年，一艘由油轮改装而成的货船“理想X号”（Ideal X）载着58个金属箱，从新泽西州启航前往得克萨斯州。麦克莱恩计算过，用这种方式装货的成本只占传统方法的一小部分。

D Success was far from immediate远非立刻见效. Ports had to invest投资 in huge cranes and large areas of flat land where boxes could be stored. Shipping companies, railways and trucking firms all used boxes of different sizes, so a container from one company often would not fit on another company’s ship or train. It was not until international standards标准 were agreed in the late 1960s that containers could move smoothly between ships, trains and lorries卡车（英式） anywhere in the world.

【D 段译文】成功远非一蹴而就。港口必须投资购置巨型起重机，并建设大片平坦的场地来堆放集装箱。当时的航运公司、铁路公司和卡车货运公司使用的箱子尺寸各不相同，因此一家公司的集装箱往往装不上另一家公司的船或火车。直到20世纪60年代末国际标准达成一致，集装箱才得以在世界各地的轮船、火车和卡车之间顺畅转运。

E Once these obstacles were overcome障碍被克服, the effects were dramatic巨大的；显著的. Ships could be unloaded in hours rather than days, and insurance保险 costs fell because sealed密封的 boxes were far harder to steal from. Manufacturers no longer needed to be located near their customers, which helped make global supply chains供应链 possible. Some researchers argue that containers did more to increase world trade than all the trade agreements signed during the same period, although this claim is difficult to prove.

【E 段译文】一旦这些障碍被克服，产生的效果是惊人的。轮船卸货只需几个小时而不再是几天，而且由于密封的箱子极难被盗，保险费用也随之下降。制造商不再需要把工厂建在顾客附近，这帮助促成了全球供应链的形成。一些研究人员认为，集装箱对全球贸易增长的推动作用超过了同一时期签署的所有贸易协定之和，尽管这一说法很难用证据直接证实。

F Not everyone benefited, however. Thousands of dock workers lost their jobs, and traditional port areas in cities such as London and New York went into decline衰落 as shipping moved to new, larger terminals码头；集装箱码头区 further from the city centre. 【F 段译文】然而，并非每个人都从中受益。成千上万的码头工人失去了工作；随着航运业务迁移到远离市中心的新建大型集装箱码头，伦敦、纽约等城市的传统港区逐渐走向衰落。此后，许多老港区被重新开发成了写字楼和高档住宅。如今，这一行业面临着新的挑战，包括巨型集装箱船造成的污染，以及单条运河堵塞就可能打乱全球货运的风险。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. FALSE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Its importance lies not in any clever technology but in a simple idea…

原文说重要性不在技术，而在一个简单的想法，和题目正好相反，FALSE。

2. TRUE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…in many ports the cost of handling goods was greater than the cost of carrying them across the ocean.

handling = loading and unloading，greater than = cost more，TRUE。

3. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…an American who owned a trucking company rather than a shipping line.

只说他开卡车公司，没说当过码头工人。别脑补，NOT GIVEN。

4. TRUE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：In 1956 a converted oil tanker, the Ideal X…

converted oil tanker 改装的油轮，说明它原本是运油的，TRUE。

5. TRUE（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：…a container from one company often would not fit on another company’s ship or train.

not compatible = would not fit，TRUE。

6. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Many of these old docklands have since been redeveloped into offices and expensive housing.

原文只说改成办公楼和住宅，没提公园。典型干扰，NOT GIVEN。

7. B（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Frustrated by the hours his drivers spent waiting at the docks…

wasted time = hours spent waiting。theft 在 B 段出现过，是干扰。

8. C（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：It was not until international standards were agreed in the late 1960s…

standards 标准 = agreed sizes。not until 引出真正的原因。

9. B（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…insurance costs fell because sealed boxes were far harder to steal from.

because 后面就是原因，harder to steal from = less likely to be stolen。

10. C（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…although this claim is difficult to prove.

difficult to prove = hard to show that it is true。注意 although 后的作者态度。

11. supply chains（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…which helped make global supply chains possible.

helping to create = helped make … possible。两个词 supply chains。

12. terminals / larger terminals（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…as shipping moved to new, larger terminals further from the city centre.

new 已经在题干里，填 terminals，填 larger terminals 也算对。

13. housing（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…redeveloped into offices and expensive housing.

turned into = redeveloped into，填 housing。

14. pollution（出处：Paragraph F）
