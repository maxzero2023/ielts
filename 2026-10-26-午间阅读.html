10月26日 午间阅读 G类 Section 1

来源：自编练习。官方 Modified Large Print 样题上周已经全部做完，ielts.org 其他 G 类样题只有在线答题版，离线拿不到原文，所以今天这篇按 G 类 Section 1 的风格自己写（日常生活类短文：课程广告加图书馆通知），题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。第1周打基础，不限时精读，参考用时 20 分钟。

做题要求
  1. Section 1 是 G 类最简单的一部分，考的是日常信息快速定位。今天两种题型：信息匹配（1–8）和判断题 TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN（9–14）。
  2. 信息匹配：先读题，圈出关键词（single day、employer、medical），再回五段广告里找同义替换。字母可以重复用。
  3. 判断题：按顺序出题，一题对应一两句。FALSE 是原文说反了，NOT GIVEN 是原文根本没说。看到 definitely、always、only 这类绝对词要特别小心。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：定位错 / 同义替换没认出 / 被干扰项带偏 / F 和 NG 分不清。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

A Beginners’ Photography

Saturday mornings, 9 am–12 noon, 6 weeks, £120. Find out how to check oil and tyre pressure轮胎气压, replace wiper blades and change a wheel. The fee covers all tools and materials, and overalls工作服；连体服 are provided. Places are limited to ten because of the size of the workshop.

One full Sunday, 9 am–5 pm, £60. This course meets the legal requirements法律要求 for workplace first aiders. Many employers will refund退款；报销 the fee, so ask your manager for a claim form before you enrol报名；注册. The certificate is valid有效的 for three years. Lunch is not included, but there are several shops nearby.

Wednesdays, 6–8 pm, 5 weeks, £75. Ingredients cost an extra £5 per session, payable应支付的 on the day. You take home whatever you cook, so remember to bring some containers容器；饭盒. Text 2（Questions 9–14）

Greenhill Public Library: Changes to Membership

1 From 1 November, all residents居民 of the Greenhill district will be able to join the library free of charge免费. Until now, adults have had to pay an annual fee年费 of £10, while children joined for free.

2 Members may now borrow up to 12 items at a time. Books can be borrowed for three weeks, and DVDs for one week. Items can be renewed续借 online up to twice, unless another member has reserved预约；预订 them.

3 Late fees for children’s books have been removed completely. However, adults will continue to pay 20p per day for each overdue逾期的；过期未还的 item, up to a maximum of £5 per item.

4 On a trial basis试行, the library will open on Sundays from 10 am to 4 pm until the end of March. A new self-service machine near the entrance allows members to return items when the front desk is busy.

5 To join, simply bring a document showing your current address, such as a recent utility bill水电煤账单 or bank statement银行对账单. Photo identification身份证明 is not required. Lost membership cards can be replaced free of charge once a year; any further replacement补办；替换 costs £3.

Questions 9–14

Do the following statements agree with the information given in the text? TRUE 与原文一致，FALSE 与原文相反，NOT GIVEN 原文没提到。

  1. Before 1 November, adult members had to pay to use the library.
  2. Books and DVDs can be kept for the same length of time.
  3. Adults will no longer be charged for returning items late.
  4. The library will definitely continue opening on Sundays after March.
  5. Members cannot renew an item online if someone else has reserved it.
  6. New members must show a passport or driving licence.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. D（出处：Text 1 D 段）

原文：One full Sunday, 9 am–5 pm

single day = one full Sunday。其他课都是好几周。

2. B（出处：Text 1 B 段）

原文：The fee covers all tools and materials, and overalls are provided.

all equipment = all tools and materials。E 课食材要另交 £5，别选错。

3. E（出处：Text 1 E 段）

原文：You take home whatever you cook, so remember to bring some containers.

carry items home 对应 take home + bring containers。A 课也要带相机，但那不是用来带东西回家的。

4. A（出处：Text 1 A 段）

原文：…finishes with a small exhibition of students’ work in the college café, which is open to the public.

ends with = finishes with，event = exhibition，public can attend = open to the public。

5. C（出处：Text 1 C 段）

原文：Students who attend at least 90 percent of classes will receive a certificate of completion.

almost every class = at least 90 percent，rewards = receive a certificate。D 课也有证书，但跟出勤没关系。

6. E（出处：Text 1 E 段）

原文：…not suitable for people with nut allergies…

medical reasons = allergies 过敏。字母可以重复用，3 和 6 都是 E。

7. D（出处：Text 1 D 段）

原文：Many employers will refund the fee, so ask your manager for a claim form…

paid for by employer = employers will refund the fee。

8. B（出处：Text 1 B 段）

原文：Places are limited to ten because of the size of the workshop.

limit on numbers = limited to ten，room = workshop。

9. TRUE（出处：Text 2 第 1 段）

原文：Until now, adults have had to pay an annual fee of £10, while children joined for free.

以前成人要交 £10 年费，和题目一致，TRUE。

10. FALSE（出处：Text 2 第 2 段）

原文：Books can be borrowed for three weeks, and DVDs for one week.

书三周，DVD 一周，时间不同，和题目相反，FALSE。

11. FALSE（出处：Text 2 第 3 段）

原文：However, adults will continue to pay 20p per day for each overdue item…

取消罚款的只是儿童书，成人还要交，FALSE。别被第一句 removed completely 带偏。

12. NOT GIVEN（出处：Text 2 第 4 段）

原文：On a trial basis, the library will open on Sundays from 10 am to 4 pm until the end of March.

只说试行到三月底，之后会不会继续原文没说，NOT GIVEN。definitely 是陷阱词。

13. TRUE（出处：Text 2 第 2 段）

原文：Items can be renewed online up to twice, unless another member has reserved them.

unless 别人预约了，就不能续借，和题目一致，TRUE。

14. FALSE（出处：Text 2 第 5 段）

原文：Photo identification is not required.

护照和驾照都是带照片的证件，原文说不需要，FALSE。
