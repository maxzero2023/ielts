10月30日 午间阅读 G类 Section 2

来源：自编练习。官方免费 G 类样题已经用完，所以按 G 类 Section 2 的风格自己写（职场类：公司员工请假制度、居家办公规定），题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。今天是第 2 周第 3 天，基础期，不限时精读，参考用时 20 分钟。

做题要求
  1. 今天练两种新题型：特征匹配（1–7，把每句话对应到一种假期 A–E）和带选项框的摘要填空（8–14，从 A–L 里选词）。
  2. 特征匹配：先把 A–E 五段各自讲什么扫一遍，再逐题找同义替换。同一个字母可以用不止一次，有的字母可能不用。
  3. 选项框摘要填空：先看空前空后，判断要填名词、动词还是形容词，再回原文定位。选项里有 5 个干扰词，有的在原文出现过但意思不对（比如 electricity、training）。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 数字换算错（21 天 = 3 周）/ 被原文出现的干扰词骗了 / 词性不对。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Text 1：Harbourside Logistics Staff Guide to Leave

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号（A–H）可单独切换该段翻译

A Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to有权享有 25 days of paid annual leave each year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time staff receive leave on a pro rata按比例 basis. All requests must be made through the online HR portal. If you wish to take more than five consecutive连续的 days off, you should apply at least four weeks in advance提前; for shorter breaks, two weeks’ notice is enough. Up to five unused days may be carried over结转；转入下期 into the next leave year, but these must be taken by the end of March.

【A 段译文】全职员工每年享有25天带薪年假，公共假日另算。兼职员工按工作时间比例享有年假。所有申请都必须通过线上人事系统提交。如果你想连续休假超过五天，至少要提前四周申请；较短的休假提前两周通知即可。最多五天未用完的年假可以转入下一个假期年度，但必须在三月底之前休完。

B Sick Leave
If you are too unwell to work, you must telephone your line manager直属经理 before 9.30 am on the first day of your absence缺勤. Text messages and emails are not acceptable, as your manager may need to rearrange shifts重新排班 at short notice临时；仓促通知. For absences of up to seven days, you can complete a self-certification自我证明 form when you return. If you are off for longer than this, you will need to provide a note from your doctor. Staff receive full pay for the first ten days of sickness in any twelve-month period.

【B 段译文】如果你身体不适无法上班，必须在缺勤第一天上午9:30之前给你的直属经理打电话。短信和电子邮件都不被接受，因为经理可能需要临时重新安排排班。缺勤七天以内的，回来上班时填写一份自我证明表即可。如果请假超过七天，你需要提供医生开具的证明。员工在任意十二个月内，前十天病假可以拿全薪。

C Parental Leave
Employees who have worked for the company for at least 26 weeks may take up to 18 weeks of unpaid parental leave for each child, at any time before the child’s eighteenth birthday. This leave is intended to旨在；打算用于 help parents spend more time with their children, for example during school holidays. It must be taken in blocks of以……为一组 one week, and you must give at least 21 days’ notice.

【C 段译文】在公司工作满26周的员工，可以为每个孩子申请最多18周的无薪育儿假，在孩子满18岁之前随时可以休。这种假期是为了帮助父母多陪孩子，例如在学校放假期间。育儿假必须以一周为单位来休，并且至少要提前21天通知。

D Compassionate Leave
In the sad event of the death of a close family member, you may take up to five days of paid compassionate leave丧假；恩恤假. We understand that every situation is different, and further time off may be granted at your manager’s discretion酌情决定权. Any additional days will normally be unpaid or deducted from从……中扣除 your annual leave.

【D 段译文】如果不幸有直系亲属去世，你可以休最多五天带薪丧假。我们理解每个人的情况都不一样，经理可以酌情批准更多假期。额外的天数通常不带薪，或者从你的年假中扣除。

E Career Breaks
Staff with at least two years’ continuous service连续工龄 may apply for a career break of between three and twelve months, for purposes such as travel, study or caring for a relative. Your job, or a similar position, will be kept open for you. Applications should be made in writing to the HR department at least three months before the planned start date, and you will receive a decision within four weeks.

【E 段译文】连续工作满两年的员工可以申请三到十二个月的职业间歇期，用于旅行、学习或照顾亲属等。公司会为你保留原职位或类似职位。申请应在计划开始日期前至少三个月以书面形式提交给人力资源部，你会在四周内收到决定。

Text 2：Harbourside Logistics Working from Home Policy

F Who can apply?
Employees who have completed their six-month probation试用期 period may apply to work from home for up to two days a week. Unfortunately, this option is not available to warehouse staff or to those in roles that involve dealing with customers face to face面对面. Requests should be discussed with your line manager first. Every new arrangement starts with a three-month trial试行期, after which it will be reviewed.

【F 段译文】已经度过六个月试用期的员工可以申请每周最多两天居家办公。遗憾的是，仓库员工以及需要当面接待客户的岗位不适用这一安排。申请应先和你的直属经理沟通。每一项新的安排都从三个月的试行期开始，试行期结束后会进行评估。

G Equipment and costs
The company will provide a laptop and a headset. You are responsible for your own desk and chair, although you may claim a one-off一次性的 allowance补贴 of £150 towards a suitable office chair. We also pay £6 a week towards heating and electricity, but home internet costs will not be reimbursed报销.

【G 段译文】公司会提供一台笔记本电脑和一副耳机。办公桌和椅子由你自己负责，不过你可以申请一次性150英镑的补贴，用来购买合适的办公椅。公司每周还会补贴6英镑的取暖和电费，但家里的网费不予报销。

H Rules for home workers
When working from home, you must be contactable能联系上的 by phone or video call during core hours核心工作时间, which are 10 am to 3 pm. All home workers are expected to come into the office on Tuesdays for team meetings. To protect customer data, confidential机密的 documents must never be printed at home, and you should lock your screen whenever you leave your desk. All staff must also complete an online data security course before their first day at home.

【H 段译文】居家办公时，你必须在核心工作时间（上午10点到下午3点）能通过电话或视频联系上。所有居家办公的员工每周二都要到办公室参加团队会议。为了保护客户数据，保密文件绝不能在家打印，每次离开座位都要锁屏。所有员工还必须在第一天居家办公之前完成一门线上数据安全课程。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. B（Sick leave）（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…you will need to provide a note from your doctor.

a document from a medical professional = a note from your doctor。

2. C（Parental leave）（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：It must be taken in blocks of one week…

in periods of a whole week = in blocks of one week。

3. A（Annual leave）（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next leave year…

saved and used the following year = carried over into the next leave year。

4. D（Compassionate leave）（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：…further time off may be granted at your manager’s discretion.

at your manager’s discretion 就是经理说了算，extra time off = further time off。

5. E（Career breaks）（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Staff with at least two years’ continuous service may apply…

two years’ continuous service = worked for two years。C 段是 26 周，别混。

6. C（Parental leave）（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…you must give at least 21 days’ notice.

21 天就是三周。A 段是两周或四周，都不是三周，这题考数字换算。

7. B（Sick leave）（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：Text messages and emails are not acceptable…

not allowed = not acceptable。同一个字母可以用两次，这题又是 B。

8. A（probation）（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Employees who have completed their six-month probation period…

finished = completed，空后是 period，填 probation。

9. B（customers）（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…roles that involve dealing with customers face to face.

meeting … in person = dealing with … face to face，填 customers。suppliers 原文没出现。

10. C（trial）（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Every new arrangement starts with a three-month trial…

begins with = starts with，填 trial。

11. D（allowance）（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…you may claim a one-off allowance of £150 towards a suitable office chair.

single = one-off，填 allowance。salary 是工资，不对。

12. E（internet）（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…but home internet costs will not be reimbursed.

paid back = reimbursed。electricity 是干扰项，原文说电费公司补贴 6 英镑。

13. F（core）（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…during core hours, which are 10 am to 3 pm.

空后是 hours，填 core。

14. G（print）（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…confidential documents must never be printed at home…

must not = must never，papers = documents，填 print。training 是另一句里的干扰项，share 原文没说。
