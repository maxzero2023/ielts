11月06日 午间阅读 G类 Section 1

来源：自编练习。官方免费 G 类样题前几天已经做完，所以按 G 类 Section 1 的风格自己写（日常生活类：五则短租公寓广告、区政府垃圾回收通知），题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。今天是第 3 周第 3 天，基础期，不限时精读，参考用时 20 分钟。

做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：信息匹配（1–7，选是哪则广告，字母可以重复用）和笔记填空（8–14）。都是 Section 1 高频题。
  2. 匹配题方法：先把 7 个题干的关键词圈出来（student card、children、reception、seven nights、wheelchair、parking、airport），再一则一则读广告，读到就回去对号，不要一题一题来回翻全文。
  3. 8–14 字数限制是 ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER，只填一个词或一个数字，超字数算错。题目里已经给了的词（比如 two、Road、hours）不要重复写。
  4. 小心干扰：同一段里会出现好几个时间（weekly、two weeks、24 hours、five working days），还有 blue / grey / green 这种颜色替换，看清题目问的是哪个容器、哪个数字。
  5. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 被相反信息干扰 / 超字数 / 看错对象。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Questions 8–14

Complete the notes below. Choose ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER from Text 2 for each answer.

Greenfield Borough recycling: new rules

  1. Blue bins will be emptied every two instead of every week.
  2. Green bins for food and garden waste are still collected
  3. Glass jars and metal cans go in the box.
  4. Batteries should be taken to the household waste centre on Road.
  5. Wait hours before reporting a missed collection online.
  6. Waste left beside a bin will be taken away.
  7. Book bulky-waste collection at least working days in advance.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…a valid student card brings a £20 weekly reduction.

pay less = £20 weekly reduction，学生证优惠。

2. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：Children under 12 stay free if they share a room with an adult.

do not pay = stay free。

3. C（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…done through our mobile app — there is no reception desk.

no staffed check-in desk = no reception desk。

4. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…stays are limited to a maximum of seven nights.

longer than one week is not possible = maximum of seven nights。

5. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：…designed for guests with limited mobility… wheelchair-accessible shower…

use a wheelchair 对应 wheelchair-accessible / limited mobility。

6. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：…free parking for two cars.

at no extra cost = free parking。别选 A，A 只提火车站，没提停车。

7. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：A free airport transfer can be arranged if you book at least 48 hours in advance.

airport pickup = airport transfer，booked early enough = at least 48 hours in advance。

8. weeks（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Blue bins … will be emptied every two weeks instead of every week.

题目已有 every two，只填 weeks。

9. weekly / every week（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Green bins for food and garden waste will continue to be collected weekly.

still = continue，填 weekly 或 every week。

10. grey / gray（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：Glass jars and metal cans must go in the grey box provided separately.

填 grey（美式 gray 也接受）。注意不是 blue bin。

11. Mill（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…take them to the household waste centre on Mill Road.

题目已有 Road，只填 Mill。

12. 24 / twenty-four / twenty four（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…please wait 24 hours before reporting it online.

填 24。

13. not（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…any waste left outside a bin will not be taken away.

题目已有 be taken away，填 not。

14. five / 5（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：Book a bulky-waste collection … at least five working days beforehand.

in advance = beforehand，填 five 或 5。别被 24 干扰。
