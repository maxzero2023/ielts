答案与解析（先做完再点开）

15. objectives

原文：…it is about the objectives of the organization, and being aware of these is essential for employees.

organization = company，understand = being aware of。注意原文是复数 objectives，填单数算错。

16. cooperation

原文：Whether you work on a specific job or are in a specific department, there has to be cooperation within the team.

there has to be = is essential；空在句首要填名词，cooperation。

17. success

原文：All your hard work is helping to contribute to the success of the business in general.

hard work = efforts，business = company，bring success to = contribute to the success of。

18. approach

原文：A valuable team leader takes the time to contemplate the ideal approach for creating an efficient team…

contemplate = consider，ideal = best，creating an efficient team = building a strong team。

19. strategies

原文：…you should recognize the strategies needed in order to do the work well and achieve daily production goals.

achieve daily production goals = meet each day’s targets；空前是 Good、后面动词 help 没加 s，所以要复数 strategies。

20. knowledge

原文：When there are issues, you should know which people on your team have the knowledge to address them.

issues = problems，address = solve，know which people = identify who。

21. updates

原文：…everyone must pay attention to any significant manufacturing updates.

pay attention to = be aware of，significant = important，manufacturing = production。

22. confidence

原文：Many people have more confidence because of participating in our association.

more confidence = confidence will increase。

23. investment

原文：Joining Speaking with Ease is a worthwhile investment you can make for yourself.

worthwhile = good，“为将来”对应投资的概念，填 investment。

24. feedback

原文：Not only does the instructor evaluate you, but also members give you feedback… This … is a key part of the program’s popularity.

key part of popularity = popularity is mainly due to；方法就是给反馈 feedback。

25. conference

原文：The final exercise the club features is a conference which is held once a year…

held once a year = annual；一个陷阱是 exercise，但空前有 annual，所指的是 conference。

26. date

原文：…email the club director and request a date to attend.

request = ask for，attend = go to a meeting。

27. introduction / presentation

原文：…you will be expected to give a short introduction of yourself.

short = brief，of yourself = personal。官方答案也接受 presentation（原文下一句 presentation style）。
