做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：判断题 TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN（1–7，对应 Text 1）和句子填空（8–14，对应 Text 2，每空 NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS，必须用原文的词）。
  2. 判断题：和原文意思一致选 TRUE，和原文矛盾选 FALSE，原文根本没提选 NOT GIVEN。别用常识补信息，原文没写就是 NOT GIVEN。
  3. 句子填空：先看空前空后猜词性，再按顺序回原文找同义替换，抄原词，注意拼写和字数。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：FALSE 和 NOT GIVEN 分不清 / 没注意 most、all 这类限定词 / 同义替换没认出 / 拼写或超字数。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

Text 1：Greenway Supermarkets: Information for New Store Assistants

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落编号（A–G）可单独切换该段翻译

A Your first week
Every new store assistant begins with a three-day induction入职培训. On your first morning, please report to向……报到 the customer service desk at 8.45 am, where you will be given your ID badge and a locker key. For the rest of the week you will shadow跟着（某人）学习 an experienced colleague, who will show you how each department works. You will not be asked to work on the tills收银台 on your own until you have completed this period.

【A 段译文】每位新店员都从三天的入职培训开始。第一天早上，请在8:45到顾客服务台报到，你会在那里领到工牌和储物柜钥匙。这一周剩下的时间，你会跟着一位有经验的同事学习，由他向你介绍各个部门如何运作。在完成这段时间之前，不会让你独自操作收银台。

B Uniform
You will be issued with被发给 two shirts and a fleece free of charge免费. If any item becomes worn out穿旧的；磨损的 or is damaged while you are at work, it will be replaced at no cost to you. However, lost items will be charged at £12 each. You will need to provide your own plain black trousers and comfortable black shoes with closed toes包头（鞋）. For safety reasons, jewellery should be kept to a minimum尽量减少: a wedding ring and small earrings are acceptable.

【B 段译文】公司会免费发给你两件衬衫和一件抓绒外套。如果任何一件衣服穿旧了，或者在工作时被弄坏，公司会免费为你更换。但是，丢失的衣物每件收费12英镑。你需要自备素色黑色长裤和舒适的黑色包头鞋。出于安全考虑，首饰应尽量少戴：可以戴婚戒和小耳环。

C Breaks and staff discount
If your shift lasts longer than six hours, you are entitled to有权享有 an unpaid lunch break of 30 minutes as well as a paid 15-minute break. The staff room on the first floor has lockers, a fridge and a water dispenser饮水机. All employees receive a 20% discount on most products, although alcohol and gift cards are excluded不包括在内. Any shopping must be done outside your working hours, and you should keep the receipt收据 in case a manager asks to see it.

【C 段译文】如果你的班次超过六小时，你有权享受30分钟不带薪的午休，外加一次15分钟带薪的休息。二楼的员工休息室有储物柜、冰箱和饮水机。所有员工购买大部分商品都可享受八折优惠，不过酒类和礼品卡除外。任何购物都必须在工作时间以外进行，并且要保留收据，以防经理要求查看。

D Working on the tills
At the start and end of each shift, the money in your till will be counted together with a supervisor主管. If there is a discrepancy差额；不符 of more than £5, you must inform the duty manager值班经理 immediately and fill in a short report. Never leave your till unattended无人看管的 while it is open, even for a moment. If a customer becomes aggressive有攻击性的, stay calm and press the call button under the counter to ask for assistance.

【D 段译文】每个班次开始和结束时，你收银台里的钱都要和一位主管一起清点。如果差额超过5英镑，你必须立即通知值班经理，并填写一份简短的报告。收银台开着的时候，哪怕一小会儿也不要离开无人看管。如果顾客变得有攻击性，保持冷静，按下柜台下方的呼叫按钮请求协助。

Text 2：Greenway Supermarkets: Staff Training and Development

E Online learning
All staff have access to the Learning Hub, our online training website. New employees must complete the food safety module within their first month, as this is a legal requirement法律要求. Other modules, such as customer care and stock control, are optional可选的. All online training is done in paid time, so please agree a suitable time slot时间段 with your supervisor before you start.

【E 段译文】所有员工都可以使用我们的在线培训网站“学习中心”。新员工必须在入职第一个月内完成食品安全模块，因为这是法律要求。其他模块，比如客户服务和库存管理，可以自愿选择。所有线上培训都在带薪时间内进行，所以开始之前请先和主管商定一个合适的时间段。

F Team Leader Programme
Store assistants who have been with the company for at least twelve months can apply to join our Team Leader Programme. Places are limited, and every application must include a recommendation推荐（信） from your store manager. The programme lasts six months. Participants spend one day a week at the regional office, attending workshops on topics such as planning rotas排班表 and managing people. All travel costs are covered by the company.

【F 段译文】在公司工作满十二个月的店员可以申请加入我们的“组长培养计划”。名额有限，每份申请都必须附上店长的推荐。该计划为期六个月。参加者每周有一天在区域办公室参加研讨课，主题包括排班规划和人员管理等。所有交通费用由公司承担。

G Retail qualifications
We are happy to sponsor资助 staff who wish to study for a nationally recognised国家认可的 retail qualification. Applications are made through the Learning Hub and are reviewed every January and July. The company pays the full course fees. However, if you leave Greenway within one year of finishing the course, you will be asked to pay back half of the fees.

【G 段译文】我们乐意资助想要考取国家认可的零售业资格证书的员工。申请通过学习中心提交，每年一月和七月审核。公司承担全部课程费用。但是，如果你在完成课程后一年内离开 Greenway，你需要退还一半的费用。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. TRUE（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…report to the customer service desk at 8.45 am, where you will be given your ID badge…

receive = be given，地点也对得上，TRUE。

2. FALSE（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：If any item … is damaged while you are at work, it will be replaced at no cost to you.

at no cost = 免费，和“必须付钱”相反，FALSE。要付 12 英镑的是丢失的衣服，别看串。

3. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：You will need to provide your own plain black trousers…

原文只说自备黑裤子，完全没提必须去哪家店买，NOT GIVEN。

4. FALSE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…an unpaid lunch break of 30 minutes as well as a paid 15-minute break.

一个 unpaid 一个 paid，说两个都带薪是错的，FALSE。

5. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：The staff room … has lockers, a fridge and a water dispenser.

只列了储物柜、冰箱、饮水机，没说能不能热饭，NOT GIVEN。别用常识脑补微波炉。

6. FALSE（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：…a 20% discount on most products, although alcohol and gift cards are excluded.

most 不等于 every，还有排除项，FALSE。

7. TRUE（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：If there is a discrepancy of more than £5, you must inform the duty manager immediately…

difference = discrepancy，over = more than，reported = inform，TRUE。

8. food safety（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：New employees must complete the food safety module within their first month…

finish = complete，填 food safety，两个词正好不超限。

9. paid（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：All online training is done in paid time…

takes place in = is done in，填 paid。

10. recommendation（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…every application must include a recommendation from your store manager.

contain = include，填 recommendation。

11. regional office（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Participants spend one day a week at the regional office…

填 regional office，注意别写成 office 漏了 regional。

12. travel costs（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：All travel costs are covered by the company.

pays for = covers，填 travel costs。

13. half（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…you will be asked to pay back half of the fees.

repay = pay back，填 half。

14. learning hub（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：Applications are made through the Learning Hub…

requests = applications，填 Learning Hub。
