11月20日 午间阅读 G类 Section 2（限时）

来源：自编练习。官方免费 G 类阅读样题已经用完，按 G 类 Section 2（工作场景）风格自己写的：一家养老院给新护理员的入职须知，9 段 A–I。题型和难度对标真题，答案和解析为自编。今天是第 5 周第 3 天，强化期，阅读限时做。

做题要求
  1. 先点“开始计时”，20 分钟内做完 14 题（Section 2 文章更长，真考建议 20 分钟左右）。时间到计时条变红，继续做完，记下超了多少。做题时先别看虚线词和翻译。
  2. 两种题型：段落信息匹配（1–7，问哪一段包含某个信息，从 A–I 选，字母可以重复用）、简答题（8–14，NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER，答案必须是原文的词，超过两个词算错）。
  3. 段落信息匹配技巧：题目描述的是“信息类型”（a rule、a reason、a warning），不是原词，要先想原文会怎么说，比如 keeping information private 原文可能是 confidential。建议先做 8–14 简答题，读完全文再回来做匹配会更快。
  4. 简答题大小写无所谓，前面的 the/a 可写可不写；拼错一个字母就算错，真考也是这样。
  5. 点“检查答案”会显示得分和用时。错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 超字数 / 拼写错 / 时间不够。做完再点“显示全文翻译”对照，挑 10 个虚线词记到底部。

Riverbend Aged Care: Induction guide for new care assistants

Read the text below and answer Questions 1–14.

💡 点击或悬停虚线词看中文；点击段落字母（A–I）可单独切换该段翻译

A Welcome to Riverbend Aged Care. As a new care assistant, you will spend your first week shadowing跟岗学习 an experienced colleague, known as your 'buddy', rather than working on your own. This gives you the chance to learn the daily routines日常作息 of the residents and to ask questions in a relaxed setting. At the end of the week, you and your buddy will meet the unit manager区域主管 to decide whether you are ready to take on your own group of residents. If either of you feels you need more time, the shadowing period can be extended延长 by up to five days.

【A 段译文】欢迎来到 Riverbend 养老院。作为新护理员，你第一周不会单独工作，而是跟着一位有经验的同事跟岗学习，这位同事叫你的“搭档”。这样你有机会熟悉住户的日常作息，也能在轻松的环境里提问。这一周结束时，你和搭档会和区域主管见面，决定你是否已经可以独立负责一组住户。如果你们任何一方觉得还需要更多时间，跟岗期最多可以延长五天。

B You will be given three sets of uniform on your first day, which remain the property of仍归…所有 Riverbend. Uniforms must be washed at a temperature of at least 60°C, so we provide a free laundry service on site在院内，现场; simply leave them in the blue bags in the staff room before 10 am. For hygiene卫生 reasons, rings with stones, bracelets and watches must not be worn on the unit. A fob watch挂表（护士表）, which pins to your uniform, can be bought from reception at cost price按成本价.

【B 段译文】第一天你会领到三套制服，制服仍归 Riverbend 所有。制服必须用至少 60 摄氏度的水洗，所以我们在院内提供免费洗衣服务：只要在上午 10 点前把制服放进员工休息室的蓝色袋子里就行。出于卫生原因，在护理区不能戴镶宝石的戒指、手镯和手表。可以别在制服上的挂表能在前台按成本价买到。

C The home operates three shifts: early (7 am to 3 pm), late (2.30 pm to 10.30 pm) and night (10 pm to 7.30 am). The overlap重叠 between shifts allows time for the handover交接班, described below. Rosters排班表 are published four weeks in advance on the staff noticeboard and on the staff app. If you wish to swap a shift with a colleague, you may do so, provided只要，前提是 both of you sign the swap form and it is approved by the shift leader at least 48 hours beforehand.

【C 段译文】养老院实行三班制：早班（早 7 点到下午 3 点）、晚班（下午 2 点半到晚上 10 点半）和夜班（晚 10 点到早 7 点半）。班次之间的重叠时间用来交接班，下面会说明。排班表提前四周在员工公告栏和员工 app 上公布。如果你想和同事换班，可以换，前提是你们两人都在换班表上签字，并且至少提前 48 小时得到值班组长批准。

D At the start of every shift, the outgoing下班的，交班的 team briefs简要汇报 the incoming team on each resident's condition, including any changes in health, mood or appetite食欲. This handover takes place in the nurses' station and normally lasts 30 minutes. You are expected to arrive in time for it, and you should take notes, as you will not be able to rely on memory alone. Information discussed at handover is confidential保密的 and must never be shared outside the building.

【D 段译文】每个班开始时，下班的团队会向接班的团队简要汇报每位住户的情况，包括健康、情绪或食欲上的任何变化。交接班在护士站进行，通常持续 30 分钟。你要按时到场参加，并且应该做笔记，因为光靠记忆是不够的。交接班时讨论的信息是保密的，绝不能在楼外透露。

E If you are unable to come to work because of illness, you must phone the shift leader at least two hours before your shift starts. Text messages and emails are not accepted, because we need to arrange cover安排人顶班 quickly. If you have had vomiting or diarrhoea腹泻, you must stay away from work until you have been free of symptoms for 48 hours, even if you feel well enough to return, as these infections spread easily among older people. For absences of more than five days, a medical certificate医生证明 is required.

【E 段译文】如果你因为生病不能来上班，必须至少在上班前两小时给值班组长打电话。短信和邮件不算数，因为我们需要尽快安排人顶班。如果你有过呕吐或腹泻，必须等症状完全消失 48 小时后才能回来上班，即使你觉得自己已经好到可以上班了也不行，因为这类感染在老人中很容易传播。缺勤超过五天需要提供医生证明。

F Falls are the most common accident in aged care. If a resident falls, do not try to lift them yourself, however light they may seem. Make them comfortable, call for the nurse on duty using the red button, and stay with the resident until help arrives. All incidents, however minor, must be recorded on an incident form事故报告单 before the end of your shift. This is not about blaming staff; the forms help us to spot patterns发现规律 and prevent future accidents.

【F 段译文】跌倒是养老护理中最常见的事故。如果住户摔倒了，不要自己去把他扶起来，不管他看起来多轻。让他保持舒适，用红色按钮呼叫值班护士，并且一直陪着住户直到有人来帮忙。所有事故，不管多小，都必须在你下班前填写事故报告单。这不是为了追究员工责任，这些报告单能帮我们发现规律，预防以后的事故。

G All new staff must complete a manual handling人工搬运 course within their first month. This is a one-day practical course run in our training room on the first Tuesday of each month, and it teaches you how to use the hoists移位机，起重机 and other equipment safely. Until you have completed it, you must not use a hoist without your buddy present. In addition, a range of online modules, such as food hygiene and dementia失智症 awareness, can be completed at home, and you will be paid for the time spent on them at your normal hourly rate.

【G 段译文】所有新员工必须在入职第一个月内完成人工搬运课程。这是一天的实操课，每月第一个星期二在我们的培训室上，教你如何安全使用移位机和其他设备。完成课程之前，搭档不在场时你不能使用移位机。另外，还有一系列网上课程，比如食品卫生和失智症认识，可以在家完成，花在这些课程上的时间按你正常的时薪付钱。

H Staff are paid every two weeks, on Thursdays, directly into their bank account. Payslips are no longer posted; instead, you can view them on the staff app. Free parking is available in the car park behind the main building, but spaces at the front are reserved for专供…使用 visitors and must not be used by staff at any time. A staff bus also runs between Riverbend and the railway station, timed to meet the early and late shifts.

【H 段译文】工资每两周发一次，在星期四直接打到员工银行账户。工资单不再邮寄，你可以在员工 app 上查看。主楼后面的停车场可以免费停车，但前面的车位是专供访客用的，员工任何时候都不能停。另外还有一趟员工班车往返 Riverbend 和火车站，时间配合早班和晚班。

I Staff working a full shift are entitled to有权享有 a 30-minute unpaid meal break. A hot meal is available from the kitchen for a small charge, or you may bring your own food and use the staff room fridge and microwave. Please do not eat in residents' rooms or in the lounge, even if a resident offers to share their food with you.

【I 段译文】上满一个整班的员工有权享有 30 分钟不带薪的用餐休息。厨房提供热饭，收一点钱，你也可以自带饭菜，用员工休息室的冰箱和微波炉。请不要在住户房间或休息厅吃东西，即使住户主动要和你分享食物也不行。

Questions 8–14

Answer the questions below. Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER from the text for each answer.

  1. Where should staff put their uniforms to be washed?
  2. Who must give permission for two colleagues to swap a shift?
  3. How long does the handover usually last?
  4. After vomiting or diarrhoea, how long must staff be free of symptoms before returning to work?
  5. What should staff use to call the nurse when a resident has fallen?
  6. Which piece of equipment must new staff not use alone until they finish the course?
  7. Where can staff now see their payslips?

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. H（出处：Section H）

原文：…spaces at the front are reserved for visitors and must not be used by staff at any time.

leave their cars = parking/spaces，must not = must not be used by staff，选 H。

2. F（出处：Section F）

原文：…the forms help us to spot patterns and prevent future accidents.

a particular type of document = incident form，reason = help us to spot patterns，选 F。

3. B（出处：Section B）

原文：A fob watch… can be bought from reception at cost price.

at a low price = at cost price（按成本价），item = fob watch，选 B。

4. A（出处：Section A）

原文：If either of you feels you need more time, the shadowing period can be extended by up to five days.

extra support before working alone = 延长跟岗期再独立负责住户，选 A。G 也提到 buddy，但讲的是用移位机的规定，不是“开始独立工作前”。

5. C（出处：Section C）

原文：Rosters are published four weeks in advance on the staff noticeboard and on the staff app.

when they will be working = rosters（排班表），in advance 原词复现，选 C。

6. D（出处：Section D）

原文：Information discussed at handover is confidential and must never be shared outside the building.

keeping information private = confidential / never be shared，选 D。

7. E（出处：Section E）

原文：For absences of more than five days, a medical certificate is required.

written evidence from a doctor = medical certificate，选 E。

8. blue bags（出处：Section B）

原文：…simply leave them in the blue bags in the staff room before 10 am.

put = leave，答 blue bags。写 staff room 不对，那是放袋子的房间，题目问放进什么里。

9. (the) shift leader（出处：Section C）

原文：…provided both of you sign the swap form and it is approved by the shift leader…

give permission = approve，答 shift leader。

10. 30 minutes（出处：Section D）

原文：This handover takes place in the nurses' station and normally lasts 30 minutes.

usually = normally，答 30 minutes。注意不要写 48 hours（那是换班提前量）。

11. 48 hours（出处：Section E）

原文：…until you have been free of symptoms for 48 hours…

答 48 hours。two hours 是请假打电话的提前量，别混。

12. (the) red button（出处：Section F）

原文：…call for the nurse on duty using the red button…

答 red button。

13. (a) hoist（出处：Section G）

原文：Until you have completed it, you must not use a hoist without your buddy present.

not use alone = without your buddy present，答 hoist。

14. (on the) staff app（出处：Section H）

原文：Payslips are no longer posted; instead, you can view them on the staff app.

now see = no longer posted; instead, you can view，答 staff app。
