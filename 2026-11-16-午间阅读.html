做题要求
  1. 今天两种题型：广告匹配题（1–7，选 A–E，同一字母可用多次）和表格填空（8–14）。
  2. 匹配题方法：先划每题关键词（meal、wheelchair、children…），再去五则广告里找同义表达。小心“提到了但意思相反”的干扰项，比如 A 也提到 lunch 和 wheelchair，但一个自费、一个不适合。
  3. 表格填空字数限制是 ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER，一个词或一个数字。填之前看空前后：前面有 a 就填单数，后面已经有的词别重复写。
  4. 点“检查答案”，错题写错因：同义替换没认出 / 被干扰项骗了 / 超字数或单复数错 / 重复了表格里的词。最后点虚线词看中文，挑 10 个记到底部。

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：A packed lunch is included in the price of £42.

includes a meal = packed lunch is included，选 E。A 的午餐是 at your own cost 自费，D 的晚餐要加钱，都不算。

2. C（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：Our coach for this trip is fitted with a wheelchair lift, and the whole shopping village is step-free.

有轮椅升降机、全程无台阶，选 C。A 明确 not suitable for wheelchair users，是干扰项。

3. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：Adults £18; children under 12 travel at half price.

lower fare for children = half price，选 B。C 的 voucher 是购物打折，不是车费便宜。

4. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：Leaves 4 pm on Fridays, returning at around midnight.

午夜左右才回，最晚，选 D。A 是傍晚6点。

5. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：Numbers are limited to 20 people…

limits the number of people = Numbers are limited to 20，选 E。

6. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：The price of £35 includes your admission ticket to both the castle and the gardens.

cost of entry = admission ticket，选 A。D 的剧院票 must be bought separately，不包含。

7. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：A two-course dinner at a nearby restaurant can be added for £20.

optional meal for an extra charge = can be added for £20，选 D。

8. utility（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…bring proof of address, such as a recent utility bill.

表格里已有 bill，只填 utility，填 utility bill 就重复了。

9. three / 3（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Registration can take up to three working days.

填 three 或 3。别和 H 段的 two working days 混。

10. nurse（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…invited to a free health check with one of our nurses.

空前有 a，要用单数 nurse，写 nurses 算错。

11. ten / 10（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：A routine appointment with a doctor lasts ten minutes.

填 ten 或 10。表格后面已经有 minutes。

12. double（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…please book a double appointment.

more than one problem 对应 double appointment，填 double。

13. phone / telephone（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：For safety reasons, we cannot accept requests by phone.

NOT accepted = cannot accept，填 phone。

14. tuesday / tuesdays（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：On Tuesdays we stay open until 8 pm…

until 8 pm 对应 Tuesdays，填 Tuesday 或 Tuesdays。
