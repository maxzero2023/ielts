Text 1: Car hire in Port Elliot

Look at the five advertisements, A–E.

Pick-up取车 is from our city centre office only, open 8 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday. Each hire includes 200 km per day; any distance in excess of超过 this is charged at 25 cents per kilometre.

【B 段译文】Budget Wheels。小型车每天只要 39 澳元起，全城最低价。我们的车比大多数公司的旧一点，但每次出租前都做全面保养。只能在市中心门店取车，周一到周六早 8 点到晚 6 点营业。每次租车每天含 200 公里，超出部分每公里收 25 分。

C GreenDrive. Our fleet车队 is made up entirely of electric and hybrid混合动力的 vehicles. Customers receive a free card that gives them the first hour of charging at no cost at any of our partner stations. 【C 段译文】GreenDrive。我们的车队全是电动车和混合动力车。顾客会拿到一张免费卡，在任何合作充电站充电的第一个小时不收费。我们还会把车免费送到市区范围内任何酒店或住宅地址，租期结束时再上门取回。最短租期为两天。

D Valley Van & Ute Hire. Moving house or picking up a new sofa? Our vans and utes皮卡（澳式说法） can be hired by the hour, with a minimum of three hours. Text 2: Westbury Go Card: your guide to travelling on public transport

F The Go Card is a reusable可重复使用的 smart card that can be used on all buses, trains and ferries in the Westbury area. Fares paid with a Go Card are around 30 per cent cheaper than paper tickets. You can buy a card at any train station, at most newsagents, or online. The card itself costs $10, which is not refundable不退款的.

【F 段译文】Go 卡是一张可重复使用的智能卡，可以在 Westbury 地区所有公交车、火车和渡轮上使用。用 Go 卡付的车费比纸质车票便宜大约 30%。你可以在任何火车站、大多数报刊店或者网上买卡。卡本身 10 澳元，这笔钱不退。

G You can top up充值 your card online, at the machines in every train station, or at newsagents. If you prefer, you can set up automatic top-up, which adds $20 from your bank account whenever your balance余额 falls below $5. Please note that money added online may take up to 24 hours to appear on your card, so plan ahead before a long journey.

【G 段译文】你可以在网上、每个火车站的机器上或者报刊店给卡充值。如果愿意，也可以设置自动充值：每当余额低于 5 澳元时，就从你的银行账户充 20 澳元。请注意，网上充的钱最多要 24 小时才会到卡上，所以长途出行前要提前安排。

H You must touch on刷卡上车 with your card at the start of every journey and touch off at the end. If you forget to touch off, the system cannot tell how far you travelled, and you will be charged the maximum fare for that trip. To keep costs down for regular travellers, there is a daily cap每日封顶: once you have paid $12 in fares on a weekday, all further travel that day is free. At weekends, the cap is reduced to $6.

【H 段译文】每次出行开始时必须用卡刷卡上车，结束时刷卡下车。如果忘了刷卡下车，系统没法知道你坐了多远，这一趟就会按最高票价收费。为了减轻经常乘车的人的负担，每天有封顶：工作日一天付满 12 澳元车费后，当天之后再坐都免费。周末封顶降到 6 澳元。

I Full-time students and seniors can travel at half price. To receive this concession优惠（票价）, you need a special concession Go Card, which you can apply for online by providing proof of your eligibility资格, such as a student ID. We strongly recommend that all users register their card online. If a registered card is lost or stolen, the remaining balance can be transferred转移 to a new card, but unregistered cards cannot be replaced.

【I 段译文】全日制学生和老年人乘车半价。要享受这个优惠，你需要一张专门的优惠 Go 卡，可以在网上申请，同时提供资格证明，比如学生证。我们强烈建议所有用户在网上给卡登记。登记过的卡丢了或被偷，卡里剩下的余额可以转到新卡上，但没登记的卡没法补办。

Questions 1–7

Which company…

  2. charges extra for younger drivers?

Questions 8–14

Do the following statements agree with the information given in Text 2? Choose TRUE if the statement agrees with the information, FALSE if the statement contradicts the information, NOT GIVEN if there is no information on this.

  1. Travelling with a Go Card costs less than paying with a paper ticket.
  2. You can get your money back for the card if you no longer need it.
  3. Money that you add to your card online can be used straight away.
  4. The Go Card can be used on the bus service to the airport.
  5. Passengers who do not touch off at the end of a trip will pay more.
  6. The daily limit on fares is the same on every day of the week.
  7. There is a small fee for registering your card online.

答案与解析（先做完再点开）

1. C（出处：Paragraph C）

原文：We will also deliver your car free to any hotel or home address within the city limits…

brought to where you are staying = deliver your car to any hotel or home address，选 C。

2. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…a surcharge of $15 per day applies to drivers under 25.

charges extra = surcharge，younger drivers = drivers under 25，选 A。D 只要求驾龄两年，不收费。

3. D（出处：Paragraph D）

原文：Every hire comes with a free trolley and furniture blankets to protect your belongings.

equipment = trolley and blankets，move heavy items = moving house / sofa，选 D。

4. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…bookings must be made at least one month before the event…

book well before = at least one month before，选 E。

5. B（出处：Paragraph B）

原文：Each hire includes 200 km per day; any distance in excess of this is charged at 25 cents per kilometre.

每天含 200 公里，超了收钱，就是限制免费里程，选 B。A 是 unlimited kilometres，正好相反。

6. E（出处：Paragraph E）

原文：…or hire one of our experienced chauffeurs.

a person to drive the vehicle for you = chauffeur，选 E。这题和第 4 题都是 E，字母可以重复用。

7. A（出处：Paragraph A）

原文：…open 24 hours a day, so you can collect your car whenever your flight lands.

any time of the day or night = 24 hours a day，选 A。B 和 D 都写了具体营业时间。

8. TRUE（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：Fares paid with a Go Card are around 30 per cent cheaper than paper tickets.

costs less = cheaper，选 TRUE。

9. FALSE（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：The card itself costs $10, which is not refundable.

not refundable 就是不退钱，和题目相反，选 FALSE。

10. FALSE（出处：Paragraph G）

原文：…money added online may take up to 24 hours to appear on your card…

题目说马上能用，原文说最多要等 24 小时，相反，选 FALSE。

11. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph F）

原文：…can be used on all buses, trains and ferries in the Westbury area.

原文只说 Westbury 地区的公交，没提机场巴士，不能推断，选 NOT GIVEN。别自己脑补。

12. TRUE（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：If you forget to touch off… you will be charged the maximum fare for that trip.

pay more = charged the maximum fare，选 TRUE。

13. FALSE（出处：Paragraph H）

原文：…once you have paid $12 in fares on a weekday… At weekends, the cap is reduced to $6.

工作日 12、周末 6，不一样，选 FALSE。

14. NOT GIVEN（出处：Paragraph I）

原文：We strongly recommend that all users register their card online.

原文只建议登记，没说要不要收费，选 NOT GIVEN。
